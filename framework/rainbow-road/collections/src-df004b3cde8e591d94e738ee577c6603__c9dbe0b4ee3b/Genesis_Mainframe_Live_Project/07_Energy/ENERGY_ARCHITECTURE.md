# Energy Architecture

## 1. Physical energy

The mainframe must be engineered in ordinary physical units:

```text
joule
watt
volt
ampere
kelvin
thermal resistance
heat capacity
```

Every basin write, read, transformation, portal transaction, Guardian computation, and recovery operation has a physical cost.

## 2. Organizational energy

The current API working model contains:

```text
epsilon_MK = Delta Pi_min
```

interpreted as the minimum admissible persistence difference.

Working total persistence form:

```text
Pi = sum_d I_d B_d R_d O_d C_d
```

where the factors refer to inheritance, bandwidth, resolution, occupancy, and closure across recursive depth.

## 3. Required calibration

The hardware program must eventually produce a map:

```text
physical energy
  -> device transition
  -> basin-state change
  -> chirality reorganization
  -> persistence change
  -> organizational energy
```

The physical joule cost can vary across substrates even if the organizational transition class remains the same.
