# Open Genesis Language Questions

These questions remain intentionally unanswered.

## Primitive object

```text
Is every Genesis value a Geometric?
Is the 3+1+1 manifold itself a type?
Is a chirality byte a primitive Geometric or a local tile inside one?
Can a Geometric be empty?
```

## Color and energy density

```text
How is a site assigned to a basin?
Is byte color based on mean, weighted mean, closure, or another operator?
What happens to non-integer aggregate values?
Are mixed colors first-class states?
How are white and black represented?
Does color resolution depend on context?
```

## Chirality

```text
What exactly may change without changing chirality identity?
How are left and right inherited?
Can mixed chirality exist?
How does admissibility constrain transformations?
```

## Portal

```text
Does a portal move the original Geometric, copy it, or re-realize it?
Which levels of identity can a portal preserve?
Can portal transport transform basin organization?
How do portals compose?
What is a fabric address?
```

## Execution

```text
Is Genesis declarative, transformational, dataflow-oriented, or something new?
Does it need counted loops?
Does it instead propagate until closure?
How are choice and branching expressed?
What is the role of Guardian inference?
```

## Hardware

```text
How are six basin states physically realized?
Is Empty zero voltage, low voltage, absence, or another state?
Does one device hold six states?
Does color emerge from a coordinated group of devices?
```

No answer should be silently canonized before the architecture supports it.
