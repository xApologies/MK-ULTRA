# Genesis Architecture Decision Register v0.1

**Purpose:** Prevent unresolved concepts from being silently collapsed into false canon.

---

## DR-001 — Set-Lift versus Inheritance/History

**Status:** Open

Recovered descriptions of `3+1+1` include:

```text
XYZ + Set-Lift + Chirality
XYZ + Chirality + Inheritance/History
```

**Temporary architecture notation:**

```text
XYZ + L + Chi
```

`L` remains neutral until the relationship between Set-Lift and Inheritance/History is formally recovered.

---

## DR-002 — Basin hard-stop versus rotation

**Status:** Working synthesis

Recovered sources preserve both:

- hard-stop structural basins,
- six rotations of one underlying structure.

**Current rule:**

A Basin is dual-aspect:

```text
identity = hard-stop basin
transition_signature = rotational structure
```

Do not replace one with the other.

---

## DR-003 — White and black

**Status:** Deferred

Current language:

```text
White = structural superposition / closure expression
Black = neon underside / nonprojected structural side
```

Executable semantics are not yet locked.

Do not encode White as an ordinary seventh basin or Black as an ordinary zero basin without further canon.

---

## DR-004 — Minimum Geometric field set

**Status:** Open for implementation review

The v0.1 IR intentionally includes more semantic fields than a minimal backend may need.

Need to distinguish:

```text
semantic obligations
mandatory serialized fields
derived fields
backend-specific fields
```

---

## DR-005 — Genesis language level

**Status:** Locked conceptually

Genesis is not assigned to one conventional level such as high-level language, bytecode, or machine code.

Current classification:

```text
architecture-native semantic language
```

Its projections include:

```text
human Genesis
Rosetta semantics
Geometric IR
Architect operator algebra
backend realization
```

---

## DR-006 — Guardian/compiler boundary

**Status:** Working synthesis

Guardian Intelligence currently performs:

```text
intent interpretation
Geometric construction
admissibility analysis
operator-plan synthesis
integrity audit
recovery
explanation
```

Implementation may separate these functions into modules, but the architecture must preserve one Trinity topology.

---

## DR-007 — Portal versus corridor

**Status:** Working lock

```text
Corridor = route/continuation object
Portal   = transaction over a route
```

Portal is the bus primitive.

---

## DR-008 — Physical portal claims

**Status:** Deferred

The architecture currently formalizes information/state transport at the model and software level.

No physical corridor actuator or substrate has been established in this package.

This does not invalidate the architecture primitive.

It separates semantic architecture from future substrate engineering.

---

## DR-009 — Quantum realization

**Status:** Deferred backend

Genesis should remain semantically portable to quantum realization.

Still required:

```text
state-space mapping
unitary/isometric operator mapping
noise model
fault tolerance
measurement semantics
closure/recovery semantics
```

---

## DR-010 — Append-only operator grammar

**Status:** Recovered constraint

The Architect operator language is append-only.

New primitive operators should be introduced only when compositional reuse of existing operators cannot express the required transformation coherently.

---

## DR-011 — Serialization

**Status:** Locked principle

Serialization is never the primary object.

The arrangement/topology is primary.

Any serialization must include enough metadata to recover arrangement, adjacency, chirality, basin structure, and provenance.

---

## DR-012 — Human optimization

**Status:** Locked design preference

The architecture is AI-native.

Human readability is a control, audit, and teaching surface.

It must not constrain the internal representation when a more explicit AI-usable structure preserves function better.
