## Executive recovery

The archive contains a substantial pre-existing Vaeld ship architecture.

The strongest foundational definition is:

> A vaeld-class construct is engineered geometry - a structured region of the Black Rainbow - grown and tuned to host consciousness, computation, and propulsion at once. It is part ship, part instrument, and part living nervous system.

Source: `0.tex`, section `0.7 Vaeld, Watcher, and the Ship`.

A second technical description defines a Vaeld as:

> A bounded region of geometry that can sense, decide, and act within a larger manifold; mathematically, a higher-order solution of the geometry engine, described as a soliton in the space of possible worldweaves.

Source: `A/block_A.tex`, chapter `Vaeld-Class Constructs and the Geometry of Ships`.

This means the Genesis computer architecture is not merely a general-purpose computer accidentally placed aboard a spacecraft. The archive already positions the computer, cognitive framework, sensory system, propulsion logic, and hull as one coupled geometric system.

### 1. Engineered geometry

A Vaeld is not defined first as a metal vehicle.

It is an engineered, self-consistent region of geometry that:

1. embeds coherently in an ambient lattice;
2. maintains an internal worldweave distinct from its background;
3. supports stable information and control channels.

Sources:

- `0.tex`
- `A/block_A.tex`

### 2. Hull

The early ship model defines a **bead hull**: a contiguous outer layer tuned to perturbations in the surrounding lattice.

The later epic model generalizes this into a **coherence hull** that maintains a minimum coherence threshold at the boundary and deflects incoherent disturbances.

Sources:

- `A/block_A.tex`
- `block_r_epic/block_r.tex`


Several equations in the epic blocks are conceptual placeholders or narrative formalizations. They establish intended relationships but are not yet sufficient engineering laws.

The archive moves between:

```text
vehicle-scale engineered geometry
cosmological court of universes
narrative metaphor
mathematical operator
```

A new architecture must mark these scales explicitly rather than silently combining them.
