Conceptual Role
---------------
The blank manifold is not an atom.
It is not a populated topology.
It is not hydrogen.

It is a recursive continuity scaffold.

The manifold acts as:
- a topology substrate,
- a chirality admissibility landscape,
- a set-lift organizational structure,
- and a recursive continuity container.

---------------------

- topology population,
- chirality admissibility,

- closure topology,
- and recursive continuity stabilization.

This prevents:
- hardcoded topology drift,
- resolution dependence,
- symbolic overfitting,
