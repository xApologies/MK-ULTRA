# VOLUME V - TIME SHELL

## Chapter 46 - Projection and Intertwining Requirements

### 46.1 Projection is not yet intertwining

The recovered Time Shell record contains several projections, restrictions, and dependency arrows. None of those forms, by itself, establishes the dynamical compatibility demanded by an intertwining theorem. The central open relation is

$$
\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}U_{\mathrm{PO}}=U_{\mathrm{QM}}\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}. \tag{46.1}
$$

Equation (46.1) is EXACT_SOURCE as a stated target and STILL_UNRESOLVED as a proof obligation. Recovery Pass 06D located the equation, its parent update, and later records that continue to call the proof open. It did not recover a proof, a fully typed physical state class, or a general extension beyond the hydrogen construction.

### 46.2 The two paths that must agree

The requirement compares two orders of operation. One evolves an upstream Planck-organizational state and then projects it into a readable quantum description; the other projects first and evolves inside the quantum description. Their intended comparison can be displayed as

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{PO}}&\xrightarrow{\ U_{\mathrm{PO}}\ }\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{PO}},\\
\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}\downarrow\quad&\qquad\quad\downarrow\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}},\\
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{QM}}&\xrightarrow{\ U_{\mathrm{QM}}\ }\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{QM}}.
\end{aligned} \tag{46.2}
$$

Equation (46.2) is CURATOR_SYNTHESIS that exposes the typing obligations implicit in (46.1). The spaces and arrows are placeholders, not recovered definitions. A commutative diagram cannot be promoted from a desired equation until the domains, codomains, admissible states, and operator regularity are supplied.

### 46.3 The recovered upstream operator

The same targeted recovery retained an explicit upstream operator form,

$$
U_{\mathrm{PO}}(\Psi)=\Chi(\Psi\otimes T)\circ R_c. \tag{46.3}
$$

Equation (46.3) is EXACT_SOURCE. It names an action involving a state $\Psi$, a Time-Shell-related object $T$, a composition with $R_c$, and a map $\Chi$. Its recovery does not settle whether $T$ is the entire Time Shell, a representation, or a state parameter. Nor does it type $\Chi$, $R_c$, or the tensor product for the physical class required by (46.1).

### 46.4 The minimum typing contract

A mathematically meaningful intertwining claim requires at least

$$
U_{\mathrm{PO}}:\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{PO}}\to\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{PO}},\qquad
\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}:\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{PO}}\to\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{QM}},\qquad
U_{\mathrm{QM}}:\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{QM}}\to\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{QM}}. \tag{46.4}
$$

Equation (46.4) is CLOVER_SYNTHESIS stating a necessary type pattern. It does not identify the spaces with Hilbert spaces, algebras, bundles, or state manifolds. Those choices remain source-dependent. The equality in (46.1) also needs a declared sense of equality: pointwise, operator, weak, distributional, approximate, or equivalence modulo a gauge relation.

### 46.5 Projection admissibility

Projection cannot be defined on every upstream expression merely because an API graph names a downstream handle. Let $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{TS}}$ denote, provisionally, the class on which a Time-Shell projection is admissible. Then the admissibility guard is

$$
\Psi\in\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{TS}}\Longrightarrow \Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}(\Psi)\ \mathrm{is\ defined}. \tag{46.5}
$$

Equation (46.5) is CLOVER_SYNTHESIS. The corpus has not supplied the complete predicate defining $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{TS}}$. Persistence closure, chirality coupling, inheritance compatibility, and boundary readability are candidate ingredients, but their conjunction is not a recovered theorem.

### 46.6 Recursive occupancy projection

The assigned address `@recursive_occupancy_projection_operator` is THIN: it carries four English units, no native equation units, and no registered dependencies in coverage_v2. Its title motivates a provisional signature,

$$

### 46.7 Inheritance through projection

The assigned `@inheritance_theory` address is also THIN, with four English units and no native equations. A projection compatible with inheritance would need a relation of the form

$$
\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}\circ I_{\mathrm{PO}}
=I_{\mathrm{QM}}\circ\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}, \tag{46.7}
$$

where $I_{\mathrm{PO}}$ and $I_{\mathrm{QM}}$ are typed inheritance operations. Equation (46.7) is CURATOR_SYNTHESIS. It names the compatibility test without asserting that either operation has been recovered. The earlier horizon chapter posed the analogous restriction question; here the obligation is extended from data preservation to dynamical inheritance.

### 46.15 Chapter result

The recovered evidence establishes an exact target equation, an exact upstream operator expression, a downstream perturbation projection, and typed API dependency placement. It does not close the bridge:

$$
\boxed{\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}U_{\mathrm{PO}}=U_{\mathrm{QM}}\Pi_{\mathrm{TS}}}
\quad\mathrm{remains\ a\ stated\ but\ unproved\ requirement}. \tag{46.14}
$$

Equation (46.14) is DETERMINISTIC_STATUS_NORMALIZATION. Chapter 47 therefore approaches the quantum-field handoff as a partially recovered construction, not as a consequence already proved here.
