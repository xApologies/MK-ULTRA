# VOLUME XI - COSMOLOGY

## Chapter 89 - Bounce Criteria and Re-Expansion

### 89.1 Kinematic expansion variable

For a homogeneous background, expansion and contraction are distinguished by

$$
H=\frac{\dot a}{a}. \tag{89.1}
$$

Equation (89.1) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Contraction has negative Hubble rate, expansion has positive Hubble rate, and a candidate bounce lies at their boundary.

### 89.2 Local bounce criterion

A regular local minimum of the scale factor at time t-b is characterized by

$$
a_b>0,\qquad H(t_b)=0,\qquad \dot H(t_b)>0. \tag{89.2}
$$

Equation (89.2) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It is a kinematic criterion; it does not identify a dynamical mechanism.

### 89.3 Constraint at the turning point

In Einstein-FLRW cosmology the Friedmann constraint at a bounce reads

$$
\frac{k}{a_b^2}=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_b+\frac{\Lambda}{3}. \tag{89.3}
$$

Equation (89.3) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Flat or open backgrounds therefore need an additional effective contribution if ordinary positive-density matter alone is assumed.

### 89.4 Acceleration condition

The acceleration equation requires

$$
\left.\frac{\ddot a}{a}\right|_{t_b}
=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\rho_b+3p_b)+\frac{\Lambda}{3}>0. \tag{89.4}
$$

Equation (89.4) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It turns the kinematic bounce test into a constraint on the total effective source.

### 89.5 Flat-background energy condition

For a flat Einstein background without a cosmological constant, a smooth bounce implies

$$
\rho_b=0,\qquad \rho_b+p_b<0. \tag{89.5}
$$

Equation (89.5) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS under the stated assumptions. Modified gravity, nonzero curvature, or additional effective sectors change the implication.

### 89.6 Raychaudhuri test

For a hypersurface-orthogonal timelike congruence,

$$
\dot\theta=-\frac{1}{3}\theta^2-\sigma_{\mu\nu}\sigma^{\mu\nu}
-R_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu. \tag{89.6}
$$

Equation (89.6) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Defocusing requires the curvature or effective-source term to overcome the nonpositive expansion and shear contributions.

### 89.7 Illustrative modified constraint

A commonly studied nonsingular effective form is

$$
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho\left(1-\frac{\rho}{\rho_c}\right)-\frac{k}{a^2}. \tag{89.7}
$$

Equation (89.7) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS as an illustrative parameterization. It is not assigned to MK 43 Ultra without a recovered action, reduction, critical-density definition, and proof.

### 89.8 Near-bounce expansion

When the first nonzero local term is quadratic,

$$
a(t)=a_b\left[1+\frac{1}{2}\dot H_b(t-t_b)^2
+O\!\left((t-t_b)^3\right)\right]. \tag{89.8}
$$

Equation (89.8) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Positive dot-H at the turning point yields local re-expansion.

### 89.9 Re-expansion branch

The post-bounce branch satisfies

$$
t>t_b:\qquad H(t)>0,\qquad a(t)>a_b. \tag{89.9}
$$

Equation (89.9) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. A complete theory must also establish stability and the duration of the expanding branch.

### 89.10 Nonsingularity criterion

A regular bounce requires finite curvature invariants, for example

$$
\left|R\right|<\infty,
\qquad
\left|R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}\right|<\infty,
\qquad
\left|R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\right|<\infty. \tag{89.10}
$$

Equation (89.10) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. A positive minimum scale factor is not by itself enough to prove geodesic completeness.

### 89.11 Perturbation handoff

The information carried across a bounce can be typed as a transfer map

$$
\mathcal T_b:
(\mathcal R_k,S_k,h_k)_{\mathrm{contract}}
\longrightarrow
(\mathcal R_k,S_k,h_k)_{\mathrm{expand}}. \tag{89.11}
$$

Equation (89.11) is DETERMINISTIC_TYPE_NORMALIZATION. It separates scalar curvature, entropy, and tensor modes without asserting a recovered transfer matrix.

### 89.12 Entropy accounting

For a closed effective description, a generalized second-law test may be written

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{matter}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{horizon}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{native}}\ge 0. \tag{89.12}
$$

Equation (89.12) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS as an accounting inequality. The native entropy term requires a definition and state-counting measure.
