## Chapter 62 - Einstein Sector and Stress-Energy

### 62.1 The dynamical threshold

Curvature becomes a gravitational field equation only when geometry is related to a source sector:

$$
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=\kappa T_{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\kappa=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}. \tag{62.1}
$$

Equation (62.1) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. The Einstein tensor, cosmological term, metric, and stress-energy tensor occupy different mathematical roles. The equation does not identify geometry with matter; it constrains them through a covariant relation.

### 62.2 Einstein-Hilbert action

For a metric theory with cosmological constant, the gravitational action is

$$
S_{\mathrm{EH}}[g]
=\frac{c^3}{16\pi G}
\int_M (R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x. \tag{62.2}
$$

Equation (62.2) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. @einstein_hilbert_action has MEDIUM coverage and a dense dependency neighborhood, but a familiar title does not by itself establish the action as the complete native MK-43 dynamics.

### 62.3 Stress-energy from matter variation

Given a matter action \(S_{\mathrm m}[g,\psi]\), define

$$
T_{\mu\nu}
=-\frac{2}{\sqrt{-g}}
\frac{\delta S_{\mathrm m}}{\delta g^{\mu\nu}}. \tag{62.3}
$$

Equation (62.3) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS for the displayed sign convention. Stress-energy is therefore a metric response of the matter action. A proposed MK-43 source must specify its fields, action or constitutive rule, and variational dependence before it can be inserted as \(T_{\mu\nu}\).

### 62.4 Metric variation

For the total action \(S=S_{\mathrm{EH}}+S_{\mathrm m}\), stationary variation gives

$$
\delta S=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
-\kappa T_{\mu\nu}=0, \tag{62.4}
$$

after the required boundary treatment. Equation (62.4) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. This action-to-equation implication is conditional on differentiability, admissible variations, and boundary conditions.

### 62.5 Conservation compatibility

The contracted Bianchi identity implies

$$
\nabla^\mu G_{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad
\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}=0. \tag{62.5}
$$

Equation (62.5) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS when Equation (62.1) holds with constant \(\Lambda\) and \(G\). Any additional source sector must be compatible with this covariant conservation law or explicitly modify the geometric side.

### 62.6 Riemann tensor

The curvature of the Levi-Civita connection is

$$
R^{\rho}{}_{\sigma\mu\nu}
=\partial_\mu\Gamma^{\rho}{}_{\nu\sigma}
-\partial_\nu\Gamma^{\rho}{}_{\mu\sigma}
+\Gamma^{\rho}{}_{\mu\lambda}\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\nu\sigma}
-\Gamma^{\rho}{}_{\nu\lambda}\Gamma^{\lambda}{}_{\mu\sigma}. \tag{62.6}
$$

Equation (62.6) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. @riemann_tensor is the most structurally connected handle in this chapter, with 813 English units, seven indexed equations, and 921 dependencies.

### 62.7 Riemann symmetries

For a Levi-Civita connection,

$$
R_{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}
=-R_{\sigma\rho\mu\nu}
=-R_{\rho\sigma\nu\mu},
\qquad
R_{\rho[\sigma\mu\nu]}=0. \tag{62.7}
$$

Equation (62.7) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. These algebraic identities are typing constraints on any object described as a Riemann tensor. A generic four-index array is not sufficient.

### 62.8 Linearized Einstein tensor

On a flat background, the first-order Einstein tensor is

$$
\begin{aligned}
G^{(1)}_{\mu\nu}[h]
=\frac12\big(&
\partial_\rho\partial_\mu h^{\rho}{}_{\nu}
+\partial_\rho\partial_\nu h^{\rho}{}_{\mu}
-\Box h_{\mu\nu}
-\partial_\mu\partial_\nu h\\
&-\eta_{\mu\nu}\partial_\rho\partial_\sigma h^{\rho\sigma}
+\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box h\big).
\end{aligned} \tag{62.8}
$$

Equation (62.8) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. @linearized_einstein_tensor has MEDIUM coverage with 628 English units, six equations, and 657 dependencies. Its scope is perturbative and background-dependent.

### 62.9 Linearized gauge freedom

An infinitesimal coordinate change generated by \(\xi_\mu\) acts as

$$
h_{\mu\nu}
\longmapsto
h'_{\mu\nu}
=h_{\mu\nu}
-\partial_\mu\xi_\nu
-\partial_\nu\xi_\mu. \tag{62.9}
$$

Equation (62.9) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Gauge-related perturbations describe the same linearized geometry. A projection output must therefore be interpreted modulo the relevant gauge relation unless a gauge is fixed.

### 62.10 Trace reversal

Define the trace-reversed perturbation by

$$
\bar h_{\mu\nu}
=h_{\mu\nu}
-\frac12\eta_{\mu\nu}h,
\qquad
h=\eta^{\rho\sigma}h_{\rho\sigma}. \tag{62.10}
$$

Equation (62.10) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. The bar here denotes trace reversal, not the background metric notation used in the preceding chapter; the roles are fixed by tensor type and context.

### 62.11 Harmonic gauge

The harmonic gauge condition is

$$
\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0. \tag{62.11}
$$

Equation (62.11) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It simplifies the linearized Einstein tensor but does not remove all physical degrees of freedom or prove that a native projection automatically lands in this gauge.

### 62.12 Sourced weak-field equation

In harmonic gauge, the field equation becomes

$$
\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}
=-\frac{16\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}. \tag{62.12}
$$

Equation (62.12) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS in flat-background linearized gravity. The vacuum equation follows only where \(T_{\mu\nu}=0\); the radiative sector is developed in the next chapter.

### 62.13 Einstein and Newtonian limit

For a weak, slowly varying field,

$$
g_{00}\simeq-\left(1+\frac{2\Phi}{c^2}\right),
\qquad
\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho. \tag{62.13}
$$

Equation (62.13) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS under the Newtonian-limit assumptions. @einstein_limit has MEDIUM coverage but only one registered dependency, so it must be treated as a scoped limit rather than a complete reduction theorem encoded by dependency count alone.

### 62.15 Source-sector bookkeeping

Distinct contributions may be organized as

$$
T^{\mathrm{total}}_{\mu\nu}
=T^{\mathrm{matter}}_{\mu\nu}
+T^{\mathrm{field}}_{\mu\nu}
+T^{\mathrm{effective}}_{\mu\nu}, \tag{62.15}
$$

### 62.16 Boundary completion

\int_{\partial M}K\sqrt{|h|}\,d^3x, \tag{62.16}
