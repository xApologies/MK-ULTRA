### 120.5 Evidence combination

For compatible independent estimates, an inverse-variance combination is

$$
\widehat\mu=\frac{\sum_i w_i\widehat\theta_i}{\sum_i w_i},\qquad w_i=\frac{1}{s_i^2}. \tag{120.5}
$$

Equation (120.5) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Compatibility and independence must be assessed rather than assumed.

### 120.6 Heterogeneity

Between-study dispersion can be diagnosed by

$$
Q=\sum_i w_i(\widehat\theta_i-\widehat\mu)^2. \tag{120.6}
$$

Equation (120.6) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Heterogeneity may reflect model variation, population differences, measurement choices, or bias.

### 120.7 Reproducibility probability

$$

$$

### 120.8 Falsification region

$$
\mathcal F_\alpha=\{D:T(D)\ge c_\alpha\}. \tag{120.8}
$$

Equation (120.8) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. The rejection region is meaningful only for a frozen model, sampling distribution, and error policy.

### 120.14 Replication and duplication

Reusing identical files does not constitute an independent replication,

$$
\mathrm{Duplicate}(D,P,I)\;\not\Rightarrow\;\mathrm{IndependentReplication}. \tag{120.14}
$$

### 120.15 Falsifiability and falsification

$$

Equation (120.15) is EVIDENCE_BOUNDARY. Potential failure conditions and observed failure must remain separate records.
