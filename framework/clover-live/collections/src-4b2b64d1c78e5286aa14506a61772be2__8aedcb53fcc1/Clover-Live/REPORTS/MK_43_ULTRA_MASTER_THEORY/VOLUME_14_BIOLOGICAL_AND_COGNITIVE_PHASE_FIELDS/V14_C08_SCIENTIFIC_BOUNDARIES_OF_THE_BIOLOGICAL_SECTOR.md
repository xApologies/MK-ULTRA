### 113.6 Bayesian model comparison

$$

$$

Equation (113.6) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Priors, marginal likelihoods, model misspecification, and sensitivity must be disclosed.

### 113.7 Out-of-sample validation

$$

Equation (113.7) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Training-set explanation does not establish generalization across individuals, settings, instruments, or scales.

Equation (113.9) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. The specific error-control procedure should match the dependence structure and scientific decision problem.
