### 105.2 Unitary preservation

For closed quantum evolution,

$$
\rho'=U\rho U^\dagger,
\qquad
U^\dagger U=UU^\dagger=I. \tag{105.2}
$$

Equation (105.2) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It gives a strict conservation setting when source and target share one Hilbert space.

### 105.3 Isometric encoding

An information-preserving embedding obeys

$$
V:\mathcal H_X\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_Y,
\qquad
V^\dagger V=I_X. \tag{105.3}
$$

Equation (105.3) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. An isometry may preserve distinguishability on its image without being surjective.

### 105.4 Trace-preserving channel

A general quantum channel satisfies

$$
\mathcal E(\rho)=\sum_aK_a\rho K_a^\dagger,
\qquad
\sum_aK_a^\dagger K_a=I. \tag{105.4}
$$

Equation (105.4) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Trace preservation conserves total probability, not necessarily recoverable information.

### 105.5 Entropy under unitary evolution

Unitary conjugation preserves the spectrum and therefore

$$
S(U\rho U^\dagger)=S(\rho). \tag{105.5}
$$

Equation (105.5) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. A projection that discards degrees of freedom need not satisfy this equality.

### 105.6 Data-processing inequality

For a channel,

$$
D(\rho\Vert\sigma)\ge
D(\mathcal E(\rho)\Vert\mathcal E(\sigma)). \tag{105.6}
$$

Equation (105.6) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It shows that operational distinguishability cannot increase under processing.

### 105.7 Exact recovery condition

Information is exactly recoverable on a code space when

$$
(\mathcal R\circ\mathcal E)(\rho)=\rho
\quad\text{for every admissible }\rho. \tag{105.7}
$$

Equation (105.7) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Conservation through a noninvertible representation requires a restricted domain and an explicit decoder.

### 105.8 Local conservation current

A continuous information-like density can satisfy

$$
\partial_t q+\boldsymbol\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_q=0. \tag{105.8}
$$

Equation (105.8) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS as a continuity template. Whether information admits this local density and current is a separate physical question.

### 105.9 Coarse-graining boundary

For a coarse-graining map,

$$
\bar x=\Pi(x),
\qquad
\Pi(x_1)=\Pi(x_2)\ \text{may hold with}\ x_1\ne x_2. \tag{105.9}
$$

Equation (105.9) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Many-to-one projection loses microscopic distinguishability unless extra records preserve the fibers.

### 105.10 Information-loss kernel

$$

Equation (105.10) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Exact conservation requires either a trivial kernel on admissible differences or auxiliary data sufficient for recovery.
