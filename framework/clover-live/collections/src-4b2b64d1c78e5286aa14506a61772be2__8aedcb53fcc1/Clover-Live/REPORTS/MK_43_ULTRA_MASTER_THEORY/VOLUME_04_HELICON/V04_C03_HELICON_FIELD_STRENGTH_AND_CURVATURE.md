### 35.2 Recovered curvature

The exact source defines the antisymmetric derivative

\[
\mathcal{F}_{ij}=\partial_iH_j-\partial_jH_i.
\tag{35.2}
\]

Equation (35.2) is EXACT_RECOVERED_EQUATION hel-eq-07, from the same coherent source unit as (35.1). It is the strongest recovered field-strength formula attached to the present chapter. Its form is compatible with the coordinate components of an exterior derivative, but that compatibility does not by itself supply dynamics, gauge symmetry, units, normalization, or an action principle.

### 35.3 Differential-form reading

For a sufficiently regular one-form, the coordinate expression can be written conventionally as

\[
\mathcal{F}=d\mathcal{H}
=\frac{1}{2}\mathcal{F}_{ij}\,dx^i\wedge dx^j.
\tag{35.3}
\]

Equation (35.3) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It gives a typed differential-form reading of the exact recovered components. The factor convention and carrier manifold must remain explicit whenever this notation is used.

### 35.4 Closure identity

The nilpotence of the exterior derivative yields

\[
d\mathcal{F}=d^2\mathcal{H}=0.
\tag{35.4}
\]

Equation (35.4) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It is a differential identity under the one-form interpretation, not a recovered Helicon equation of motion and not evidence that the field obeys the dynamics of a conventional gauge theory.
