### 72.1 Spectral meaning of an energy band

For a self-adjoint Hamiltonian \(H\), an energy band is a connected subset \(B_n\) of its spectrum,

$$
B_n\subset\sigma(H)\subset\mathbb R. \tag{72.1}
$$

### 72.2 Band projectors

The spectral projector associated with a band is

$$
P_n=\mathbf 1_{B_n}(H),
\qquad
P_n^2=P_n,
\qquad
P_nP_m=0\quad(n\ne m). \tag{72.2}
$$

Equation (72.2) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. A color label may index projectors, but it must not be confused with visible color unless a measurement map establishes that interpretation.

### 72.3 Chromatic labels

Let \(\mathcal K\) be a state space and \(\mathcal A\) a finite label set. A labelled decomposition is

$$
\mathcal K=\bigoplus_{a\in\mathcal A}\mathcal K_a,
\qquad
P_a:\mathcal K\to\mathcal K_a. \tag{72.3}
$$

Equation (72.3) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. It supplies a generic organizational role for chromatic strata without asserting native dimensions, multiplicities, or physical observables.

### 72.4 Observer-dependent energy

For four-momentum \(p^\mu\) and a unit timelike observer \(u^\mu\), measured energy is
