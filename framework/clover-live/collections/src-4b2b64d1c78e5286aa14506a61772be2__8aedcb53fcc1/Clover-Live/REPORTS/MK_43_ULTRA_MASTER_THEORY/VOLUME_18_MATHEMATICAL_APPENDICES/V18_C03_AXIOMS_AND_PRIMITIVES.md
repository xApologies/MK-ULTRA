### 137.2 Axiom role

Within a declared formal system, an axiom is an admitted premise,

$$
A\in\mathcal A\quad\Rightarrow\quad \mathcal A\vdash A. \tag{137.2}
$$

Equation (137.2) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Admission inside a framework does not establish empirical truth or acceptance outside that framework.

### 137.3 Primitive role

A primitive is introduced without reduction to prior terms in the same presentation,

$$
p\in\mathcal P\quad\land\quad \neg\exists d\in\mathcal D_{<p}:d\equiv p. \tag{137.3}
$$

Equation (137.3) is DETERMINISTIC_TYPE_NORMALIZATION. It records a formal role, not metaphysical fundamentality.

### 137.4 Definition role

$$

$$

Equation (137.4) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Definitions, axioms, primitives, lemmas, and theorems therefore remain different record classes.

### 137.5 Derivation closure

The deductive closure of axioms under rules is

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal A,R)=\{\varphi:\mathcal A\vdash_R\varphi\}. \tag{137.5}
$$

Equation (137.5) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. A catalog cannot assert membership in this closure when hypotheses or inference steps are absent.

### 137.6 Independence boundary

An independence claim requires two non-derivability obligations,

$$
\mathcal A\setminus\{A_i\}\nvdash A_i,qquad \mathcal A\setminus\{A_i\}\nvdash\neg A_i. \tag{137.6}
$$

Equation (137.6) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. No such proof is inferred from address labels or coverage counts.

### 137.7 Consistency boundary

Syntactic consistency is the absence of a formula and its negation in the same closure,

$$
\operatorname{Con}(\mathcal A)\iff\nexists\varphi:(\mathcal A\vdash\varphi)\land(\mathcal A\vdash\neg\varphi). \tag{137.7}
$$

Equation (137.7) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. The title Core Axioms does not by itself supply a consistency proof.

### 137.8 Model boundary

A model satisfies a formal system when it satisfies every admitted axiom,

$$
\mathfrak M\models\mathcal A\iff\forall A\in\mathcal A,\ \mathfrak M\models A. \tag{137.8}
$$

Equation (137.8) is EXTERNAL_ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS. Model existence, physical realization, and observational validation remain separate questions.
