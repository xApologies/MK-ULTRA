## 1. System identity

The Genesis Mainframe is an AI-native computer architecture organized around chirality, structural basins, geometrics, recursive topology, and closure.

It is not a conventional computer with a novel programming language placed on top.

It is a co-designed system comprising:

```text
physical substrate
persistent configuration storage
chirality fabric
native transformations
operating topology
Genesis language
Guardian Intelligence
portal interconnect
application interfaces
```

These can be engineered as separate workstreams, but canonically they are perspectives and functions of one recursively dynamic topology.


The byte has both:

```text

```

Example:

```text

```

and:


This model is pre-formal. The aggregate rule, voltage realization, and language syntax remain open.

```text
Color : Geometric -> {
    Red,
    Orange,
    Yellow,
    Green,
    Blue,
    Violet
```

Many distinct Geometrics may share one color.

A Green-specific portal may admit all and only Geometrics whose resolved basin is Green, while the full internal Geometric remains the payload.

Candidate portal classes:

```text
color-gated
color-preserving
color-transforming
color-multiplexed
```

Color supplies a coarse routing/indexing layer.

Chirality, internal distribution, identity, Resolution, Bandwidth, and closure supply the finer admission and preservation contract.
