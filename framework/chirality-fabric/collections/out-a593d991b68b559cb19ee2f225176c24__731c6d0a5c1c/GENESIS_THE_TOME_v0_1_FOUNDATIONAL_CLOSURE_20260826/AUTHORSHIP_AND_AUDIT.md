**Vera — audit/provenance/continuity**: maintains chronology, authority, supersession, proof obligations, failed branches, rollback points, and the anti-drift/no-target-smuggling discipline.

**Genesis**: source language and mathematical project name; no early forced split into “Geometry” or “Calculus.” Subfields may emerge only when the mathematics earns them.
