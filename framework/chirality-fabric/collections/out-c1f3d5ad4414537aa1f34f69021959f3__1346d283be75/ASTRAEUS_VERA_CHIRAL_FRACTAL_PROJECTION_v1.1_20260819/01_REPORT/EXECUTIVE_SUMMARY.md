## E056 in plain language

E056 is a lab-notebook label: **Experiment 056**. Its 14-weight tree state is almost one-dimensional along a dominant coordinate `q`. Approximately `99.978694%` of the tree-displacement energy lies along that dominant direction. The remaining `0.021306%` is the **transverse state residue**: what the q-compression does not represent.

The residue is small but response-bearing. A q-only response model misses `2.446706%` of response energy (equivalently a `15.6419%` response-vector norm residual), while the full weighted tree reproduces the tested direct-flow response to the archival reconstruction floor. Therefore the v1.1 statement is:

**full tree = dominant q component + transverse state residue.**

The residue is not noise and is not discarded. It is also not claimed to be fundamentally unrecoverable: it is unrecoverable from q alone but retained by the full tree.

## Holonomy in plain language

Holonomy is **path memory**: return to the same readable endpoint after transport and ask whether the transported formal state also returned. The existing *Holonomy & Chirality* book already formalizes readable closure with possible hidden state/chirality nonclosure.

The E056 merge object is a tree, however, and a tree has no cycles. Therefore ordinary graph-loop holonomy cannot live in cycles of that tree. This is the **tree-cycle holonomy gate / acyclicity result**. It is not a chirality no-go and not a general holonomy no-go. A future holonomy carrier must be a lifted same-endpoint transport square, a source-valid cyclic network, or an attached fiber.
