# Water / Ice / Time-Shell Model

## Final active mapping

| Analogy | MK43 interpretation |
|---|---|
| water (substance/medium) | chirality / organizational grammar |
| liquid-water organization | one persistent Time-Shell organization |
| ice-cube organization | second persistent Time-Shell organization |
| interface/contact | interacting shell boundary/constraint interface |
| melting | CRD-driven relaxation of the ice shell's local chirality configuration |
| ice no longer separately present | loss of the independently persistent Time-Shell distinction |
| resulting water | one surviving/updated Time-Shell organization |

## State-count picture

Before relaxation:
`#TS = 2` — `TS_water` and `TS_ice`.

After complete relaxation/assimilation:
`#TS = 1` — `TS_water'`.

The chirality did not vanish. The separately persistent organization did.

## Why this analogy is deliberately simple
It communicates persistence, local distinction, interaction, relaxation and assimilation without assuming the physical details of a specific microscopic implementation. Do not over-literalize ordinary water thermodynamics into MK43 dynamics; the analogy is structural.
