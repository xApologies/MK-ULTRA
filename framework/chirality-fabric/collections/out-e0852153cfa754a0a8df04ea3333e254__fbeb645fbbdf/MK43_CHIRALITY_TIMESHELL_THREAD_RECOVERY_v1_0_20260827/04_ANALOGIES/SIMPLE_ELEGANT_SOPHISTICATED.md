# Simple. Elegant. Sophisticated.

Three words, one research motto.

**Simple:** keep primitives and explanations minimal.  
**Elegant:** preserve relationships, types and provenance without unnecessary machinery.  
**Sophisticated:** allow rich behavior to emerge from configuration, recursion, history, Resolution, Bandwidth, persistence and typed composition.

Recursive systems amplify bad formulation. Complexity should be earned by the structure rather than inserted at the beginning.
