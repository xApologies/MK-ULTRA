# SIMPLE. ELEGANT. SOPHISTICATED.

The primitive should remain simple enough that recursion does not amplify ambiguity.

- Four tiles form a seed.
- A seed is lifted into a byte/bite.
- Bandwidth expands the domain into a `4 x 4 x 4` block.
- Occupied tiles carry local chromatic/energy-density states.
- Larger domains resolve local color charge without erasing internal History.
- Complexity comes from relation, recursion, History, local persistence, and multiscale Resolution—not from adding unrelated primitives.
