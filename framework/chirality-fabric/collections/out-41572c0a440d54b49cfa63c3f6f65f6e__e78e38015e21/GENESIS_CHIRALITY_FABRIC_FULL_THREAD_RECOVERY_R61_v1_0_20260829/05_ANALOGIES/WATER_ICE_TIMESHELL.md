# WATER / ICE / TIME-SHELL ANALOGY

Current mapping:

- **water** = chirality as common organizational substrate;
- **liquid-water organization** = one persistent local Time Shell;
- **ice organization** = a second persistent local Time Shell;
- the two shells interact through their local interface;
- CRD may relax the ice-shell chirality organization until its persistent distinction is no longer maintained;
- after melting, two locally distinguishable shells become one water shell.

Symbolically:

`TS_water + TS_ice ->[interaction + CRD] TS_water'`.

Chirality is not destroyed. The independently persistent organization disappears.

The cup/container is intentionally left outside the current analogy because it introduces a separate boundary/containment problem.
