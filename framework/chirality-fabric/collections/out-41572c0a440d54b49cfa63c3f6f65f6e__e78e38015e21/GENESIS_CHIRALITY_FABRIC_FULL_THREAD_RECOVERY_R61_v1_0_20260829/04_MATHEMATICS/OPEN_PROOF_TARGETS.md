# OPEN PROOF TARGETS

## Priority 1 — Native multiscale update law

Construct a source-respecting `K_chi` or `U_chi` that determines operative local updates from the decorated chirality context.

## Priority 2 — Tile coupling

Determine whether a tile update:

- only changes the relation;
- changes the neighboring tile's future availability;
- forces immediate compensation;
- or forms one joint event.

## Priority 3 — Bandwidth semantics

Determine whether Bandwidth controls:

- orientation;
- phase reach;
- speed;
- generator availability;
- simultaneous update rank;
- capacity/energy-density transfer;
- or a combination.

Do not equate “Bandwidth two” with two neighboring final colors without proof.

## Priority 4 — Set-lift

Specify the exact 2D seed -> 3D byte/bite lift.

## Priority 5 — Readout composition

Compare direct fine-grained color resolution with recursively truncated tile->seed->byte->block resolution.

## Priority 6 — Canonical noncommutativity test

Find two **earned** local updates `A,B` and test `AB` against `BA` on the decorated state space.

## Priority 7 — Simultaneity

Formalize when multiple local updates are independent events, ordered coupled events, or one joint event.

## Priority 8 — Energy density

Derive the map from local occupied-tile energy density to the six-color basin/readout and determine whether color is state, telemetry, or both at different layers.

## Priority 9 — Unoccupied tiles

Determine whether unoccupied tiles carry latent chirality organization, no color, a Void/DNE marker, or another structure.

## Priority 10 — Downstream handoff

Construct `Lambda_HQ` without importing QCD/SU(3), then test whether a rank-3/non-Abelian sector emerges under projection.
