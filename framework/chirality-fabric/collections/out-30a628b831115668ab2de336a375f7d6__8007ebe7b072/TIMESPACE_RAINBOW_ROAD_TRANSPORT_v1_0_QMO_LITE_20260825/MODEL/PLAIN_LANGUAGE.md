# Plain-Language Model

The simplest picture is:

- **Time-Space** is the whole five-dimensional landscape.
- An **Address** says where a state is in that landscape and carries enough metadata to know whether it is still the same state.
- A **Corridor** is one legal route through the continuity part of Time-Space.
- A **Portal** is one transaction that uses a Corridor.
- A **Rainbow Road** is a sequence of Portals.
- A **projection/readout** is what some lower-dimensional observer or physical representation can see.

The important identity rule is:

**looking the same at the destination does not prove that two states took the same Road.**

History stays separate.

For QFT and GR, the question becomes:

**If the underlying state travels along a Rainbow Road, do its quantum and gravitational descriptions travel consistently with it?**

We can now prove the bookkeeping theorem:

If the QFT or GR representation behaves consistently on every individual Portal, it behaves consistently on the entire composed Rainbow Road.

What we have **not** derived is the actual physical law that tells QFT or GR how to propagate along every such Time-Space Road. Those future maps are called `U_Q` and `U_G`.

That is the natural place to stop instead of recursively inventing more structure.
