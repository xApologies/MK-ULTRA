## Governing ontology

Use the current five-dimensional Time-Space geometry:

`S2 --> T5 --pi--> S3`

with local fibers `Sigma_p ~= S2`.

The older physical assignment in which S4 was the universe is not used here. The newer T5/S3/S2 assignment supersedes it for this transport model.

A Corridor is the Time-Space fibration restricted to an admissible path through the S3 continuity domain.

A Portal is a transaction over an admissible Corridor.

A Rainbow Road is an ordered composition of Portals.
