## Foundational inheritance architecture

Volume I describes MK43 Ultra as a layered theory in which upstream organization is not simply renamed as familiar fields. It is projected, restricted, stabilized, and handed off through explicit mathematical objects.

The pre-geometric level includes chirality, persistence, inheritance, and connectivity. The transduction level includes Helicon, Time Shell, projections, and quantum handoff objects.

The Tome explicitly says that scalar, gauge, spinor, and Yang-Mills realizations are separate downstream model families and that MK43's distinctive task is to show how their admissible inputs are inherited from upstream organization while preserving constraints, causality, conservation, and probability.

## Chirality inheritance firewall

The chirality-language rule requires a carrier, mirror rule, admitted equivalence, representation, History, and provenance.

The chirality-to-Helicon-orientation relation exists as a scoped candidate architecture, but its exact domain, codomain, normalization, covariance, and proof are still open.
