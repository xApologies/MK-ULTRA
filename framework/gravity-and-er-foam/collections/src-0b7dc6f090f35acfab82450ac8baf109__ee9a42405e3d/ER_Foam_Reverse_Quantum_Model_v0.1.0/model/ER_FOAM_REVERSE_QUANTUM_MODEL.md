## Forward recovery

Define amplitude filters

\[

\]

and

\[
D_\Chi(o)=
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{1.05},\sqrt{0.95})_c,
& o=\text{inherit or stabilize},\\
I_c,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

Let \(\Pi_C\) project onto the six constructive operator sectors. Then

\[
|Q_{\mathrm{ER}}\rangle
=
\frac{\Pi_C D_\Chi D_T|Q_0\rangle}
{\|\Pi_C D_\Chi D_T|Q_0\rangle\|}.
\]

exactly after normalization.
