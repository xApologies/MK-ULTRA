# R72 — PROFILE-LEVEL EQUIVALENCE CLASSIFIER

## Goal

Resolve the R71 branch:
what should "structure-preserving equivalence" mean at each level of the closed recursive projection tower?

---

# 1. Exact congruence

Too strict as the universal rule.

Identity should be able to survive coordinate deformations/propagation that preserve relational/incidence structure.

Therefore exact metric congruence cannot be mandatory at every Resolution level.

---

# 2. Homeomorphism alone

Too weak.

A homeomorphism may preserve coarse topology while forgetting:
- recursive cell labels;
- higher-order incidence;
- designated Resolution data;
- History/provenance.

So bare topological equivalence cannot be the complete identity law.

---

# 3. Simplicial/cellular isomorphism

Much better.

It preserves:
- vertices/cells;
- higher-order incidence;
- recursive combinatorial organization.

It can tolerate geometric deformation while preserving the relational skeleton.

But it still does not distinguish two different ordered Histories through the same combinatorial geometry.

---

# 4. History-decorated tower isomorphism

The strongest current candidate is:

**an isomorphism/equivalence of the truncated closed recursive projection towers that:**
1. preserves simplicial/cellular incidence;
2. commutes with every bonding/projection map;
3. preserves designated Resolution/Bandwidth/identity labels;
4. transports the History witness/provenance coherently.

Call this:

`Phi_R : Tower_R(X) ~= Tower_R(Y)`.

Then:

`X ~_R Y`

iff such a History-decorated tower equivalence exists.

This is not merely "same shape."
It is:

**same closed recursive relational organization through active Resolution, with compatible projection ancestry and History witness.**

---

# 5. Canonical law candidate

Let:

`T_R(X)`

be the truncated inverse projection tower of X through active Resolution R.

Define:

`X ≡_Pi,R Y`

iff there exists:

`Phi_R:T_R(X)->T_R(Y)`

such that:

- `Phi_R` is levelwise simplicial/cellular structure-preserving;
- every projection/bonding square commutes;
- designated closed-fractal profile invariants agree;
- the History witness is carried coherently;
- Bandwidth-required admissibility labels are preserved where they are part of the active identity contract.

This closes the former R68 matching-law roadblock at the structural level.

---

# 6. Remaining branch

One branch remains, but it is now semantic/contractual rather than structural:

**Which labels/invariants are identity-essential at each profile?**

The general law says "preserve the designated identity contract."

But the exact contract can differ by projection profile.

For example:
- one profile may preserve only incidence + History;
- another may additionally require orientation;
- another may require a metric ratio;
- another may require recursive occupancy labels.

The mathematics cannot infer profile-specific identity-essential labels unless the profile declares them.

This is a legitimate parameterization, not a mathematical failure.

---

# Verdict

**R72 CLOSES THE CANONICAL MATCHING LAW TO A PROFILE-CONTRACT BRANCH.**

No failure.
No deeper mathematical roadblock in the generic theory.

The generic law is:

> Identity equivalence at Resolution R is History-decorated isomorphism/equivalence of the closed recursive projection towers through R, commuting with all projection maps and preserving the declared identity contract.

The only remaining branch is the profile-specific declaration of which invariants belong to that contract.

Stop here.
