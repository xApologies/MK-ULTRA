# R78 Roadblock

We can describe the engine and every legal kind of transition, but we have not derived the rule that decides **when to engage which dimensional level**.

Navy analogy:
the machinery knows what STOP, 1/3, 2/3, STANDARD, FULL mean and how to transition.
What is missing is the mathematically defined equivalent of the command/order or automatic governor that selects the setting.

Candidates must be derived rather than guessed:
external command, Resolution demand, Bandwidth admissibility, Rainbow Road route requirements, or a combination.
