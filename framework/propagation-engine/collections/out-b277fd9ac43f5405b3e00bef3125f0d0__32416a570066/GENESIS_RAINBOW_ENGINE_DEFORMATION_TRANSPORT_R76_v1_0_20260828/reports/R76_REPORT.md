# R76 — RAINBOW ROAD ENGINE DEFORMATION TRANSPORT

Rotation and invertible stretching preserve the generic engine identity when the recursive cell/incidence tower is preserved.

Reflection also preserves the generic structural engine class unless orientation is explicitly identity-essential.

A rank-deficient collapse is different: it may destroy same-dimensional incidence and should be typed as dimensional projection/engagement change rather than identity-preserving deformation.

This forces two distinct morphism roles:
- same-dimensional engine deformation;
- dimension-changing engagement projection.
