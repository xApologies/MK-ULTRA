# Current PSSP Clarifications — 2026-09-01

Current-authority rulings from Genesis:

1. The Binding Circle is its own mechanism. It gives the practitioner the compact/latent “spiky-ball” phase-state as part of the practitioner's admissible inherited Genesis identity.
2. PSSP is the access/projection/expansion of that latent phase-state into the active sphere/manifold.
3. The PSSP sphere/manifold is the scaffold into which energy is organized.
4. The manifold specifies what is permissible as an admissible structural path/corridor.
5. Basin requirements are structural, not power levels.
6. Example: if Fireball requires Red, Orange, and Green, that means those basin-conditioned energy structures are required to stabilize specific parts/corridors of the Fireball manifold.
7. Energy density stabilizes required corridors; basin identity does not say how powerful the Fireball is.
8. Carpenter analogy: choosing a basin for a corridor is like choosing a 2×4 or 4×4 for a frame because the structure requires that member.
9. After the manifold is realized and energetically stabilized, the practitioner consciously resolves/collapses it into the operative shaping.
