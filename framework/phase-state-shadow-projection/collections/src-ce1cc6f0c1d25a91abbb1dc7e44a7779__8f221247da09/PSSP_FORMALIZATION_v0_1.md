# Phase State Shadow Projection (PSSP) v0.1

## Canonical role

PSSP realizes a bound phase-state as the persistent Genesis manifold that defines the admissible structural corridors into which basin-conditioned energy may be organized prior to conscious shaping.

## 1. Binding and phase-state availability

Let `K` denote a practitioner's closed local Genesis identity and `Adm(K)` the configurations currently admissible to that identity.

A successful Binding Circle makes a shaping phase-state `s` available under the practitioner's inherited identity:

`K --Bind--> K[s]`

with the practitioner remaining the same recoverable identity under the relevant Genesis identity relation.

The bound phase-state is the compact/latent configuration (“spiky ball”), not yet the active manifold.

## 2. PSSP realization operator

For Genesis domain `D`, available Bandwidth `B`, and operative Resolution `R`:

`PSSP_(D,B,R) : Adm(K) ⇀ Man(D)`

and

`M = PSSP_(D,B,R)(s)`.

PSSP is a realization projection. It is not silently identified with the generic dimension-reducing `PROJECT` role in the Genesis morphism firewall.

## 3. Admissibility conditions

A candidate shadow manifold `M` is PSSP-realizable from `s` when the relevant conditions hold:

`M ∈ Reach_(D,B)(s)`

`Inv_K(M) = Inv_K(s)`

`rho_R(M)` is an admissible distinguishable readout

`Cl(M) = M`.

These conditions encode reachability, identity preservation, Resolution admissibility, and closure.

## 4. Shadow manifold

Working manifold object:

`M = (X_M, C_M, I_M, b_M, S_M)`

where:
- `X_M` = manifold carrier,
- `C_M` = internal Genesis corridor set,
- `I_M` = incidence/topological organization,
- `b_M` = corridor basin-requirement map,
- `S_M` = corridor stabilization-condition map.

The shadow manifold is the active structural scaffold. It is not the energy and is not yet the final manifested shaping.

## 5. Basin typing

Let the ordinary basin alphabet be:

`Basin = {R,O,Y,G,B,V}`.

For every corridor `c ∈ C_M`:

`b_M(c) ⊆ Basin`, with `b_M(c) ≠ ∅`.

The complete basin signature is:

`BasinSig(M) = union_(c∈C_M) b_M(c)`.

Example:

`BasinSig(M_Fireball) = {R,O,G}`

means Fireball's scaffold requires Red-, Orange-, and Green-basin structural participation. It does not define a power hierarchy.

## 6. Corridor energy-density stabilization

For a corridor `c` requiring basin set `{b_1,...,b_n}`, define a local energy-density assignment:

`e_c = (e_(c,b_1),...,e_(c,b_n))`.

Let:

`S_M(c) ⊂ R_>=0^n`

be that corridor's admissible stabilization region.

The corridor is stabilized iff:

`e_c ∈ S_M(c)`.

The whole shadow manifold is stabilized iff:

`for all c ∈ C_M, e_c ∈ S_M(c)`.

## 7. Structure versus magnitude

`BasinSig(M) != Magnitude(M,E)`.

The basin signature is a structural construction specification. Different energetic magnitudes can share the same basin signature.

## 8. Shaping handoff

After PSSP and energy stabilization, conscious shaping acts on the energized scaffold:

`Shape_(K,Q) : (M,E) ⇀ Y`

where `Y` is the operative manifested shaping.

## 9. Complete mechanism

`K --Bind--> K[s] --Access--> s --PSSP--> M --Basin Energy/Stabilization--> (M,E) --Shape--> Y`

Core separation:

`Phase State != Shadow Manifold != Energy Occupancy != Manifested Shaping`.
