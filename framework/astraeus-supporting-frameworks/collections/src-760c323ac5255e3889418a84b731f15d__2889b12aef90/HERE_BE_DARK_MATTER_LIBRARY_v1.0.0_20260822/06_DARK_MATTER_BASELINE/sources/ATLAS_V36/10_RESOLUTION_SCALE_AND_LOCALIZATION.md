# 10 — Resolution, Scale, and Localization

The Atlas is intended to map the same organizational architecture across Resolution.

Examples of localized domains may include:

```text
atomic manifold
molecular manifold
material domain
planetary domain
galactic/local spacetime domain.
```

The Atlas does not assume these are identical physical objects. It asks whether they can be represented as localized restrictions/charts of one dependency architecture under different Resolution and Bandwidth regimes.

## Local state

For a domain `D`:

```text
chi_D := chi|_D.
```

Readable representation at Resolution `R`:

```text
View_R(D) = rho_R(chi_D).
```

## Galaxy interpretation

The working physical interpretation associates selected galaxy-scale localized projections with `S2` address domains, potentially anchored to black-hole-centered organization.

This is a physical interpretation layer, not a theorem of ordinary topology.
