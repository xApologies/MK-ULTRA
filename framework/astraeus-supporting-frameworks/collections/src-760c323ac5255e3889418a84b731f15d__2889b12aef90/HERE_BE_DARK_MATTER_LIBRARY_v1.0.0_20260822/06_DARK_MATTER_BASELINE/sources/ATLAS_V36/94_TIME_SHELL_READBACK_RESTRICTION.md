# 94 — Time-Shell Readback / Restriction

## Purpose

A downstream/readable state does not generally invert uniquely to its upstream source. Readback is therefore typed as
restriction/inheritance rather than inverse transduction.

For:

```text
f : U -> V
```

and downstream state:

```text
y in F(V),
```

define:

```text
f^* y in F(U).
```

Interpretation:

> `f^*y` is the maximally inherited/recoverable source-context organization compatible with `y` under the declared
> Resolution, Bandwidth, Identity, History, and invariant contract.

## Information asymmetry

Forward realization may create/read target-local distinctions that readback legitimately discards. Therefore:

```text
f^* f_! x need not equal x as representation
```

and:

```text
f_! f^* y need not equal y as representation.
```

The relevant question is recoverable Identity/invariant compatibility, not literal equality.

## Cartesian universality target

The stronger categorical target is:

> every admissible sourceward arrow into `y` over a factorized base relation uniquely factors through the selected
> restriction lift.

This is structurally supported by the Resolution/localization branch but remains domain-specific.
