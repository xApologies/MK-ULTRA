\section*{Overture: The Moment Infinity Turns Inward}
Infinity--Spectrum Algebra revealed a cosmos of spectral constellations and luminous horizons.
But the expedition now encounters a deeper truth:

\emph{Infinity collapses. And the pattern of its collapse is geometry.}

Collapse Geometry is the gravitational heart of the Abyssalum.
If Genesis Algebra was a platform, and Infinity--Spectrum Algebra was sky,
Collapse Geometry is the pull that binds everything into form.

\section{Collapse as an Ontological Force}
Collapse is not decay but \emph{selection}. It funnels possibility into narrow spectral channels.

\[
\frac{\mathrm{d}\psi}{\mathrm{d}t}
=
-\sum_{\beta}
\lambda_\beta \hat{H}_\beta \psi,
\]

This is not time evolution but \emph{descent} toward structures that resist dissolution.

\section{Collapse Wells and Spectral Gravity}
A collapse well is:
\[
W_\beta =
\{\psi : \mathrm{Spec}(\hat{H}_\beta \psi) \to -\infty\}.
\]

Three types emerge:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Parabolic Wells}: smooth, predictable descent.
  \item \textbf{Fractal Wells}: jagged, recursive, phase-sensitive.
  \item \textbf{Spiral Wells}: spectral hurricanes winding states inward.
\end{itemize}

These wells are geometries in spectral-coordinate form.

\section{Collapse Channels: Highways Through the Abyss}
Collapse channels are minimal-action spectral geodesics:
\[
\Gamma_\beta = \{\psi(t)\;|\;\psi \in W_\beta,\; \psi \text{ minimizes collapse action}\}.
\]

They merge and structure deeper descent.

\section{Collapse Manifolds: The First True Geometric Surface}
\[
\mathcal{M}_C =
\bigcup_{\beta}
\Gamma_\beta^{\mathrm{stable}}.
\]

Collapse manifolds possess curvature, torsion, singularities, and topological transitions.
They are the first fully geometric objects since ordinary spacetime.

\section{Spectral Tension and Collapse Shear}
Collapse shear tensor:
\[
\mathcal{S}_{\alpha\beta}
=
\frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{H}_\alpha}
\left(
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}
\mathrm{Spec}_{\hat{H}_\beta}
\right).
\]

High shear indicates instability and phase tearing; low shear indicates coherence.
