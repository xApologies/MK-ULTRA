
\chapter{Ascendant Geometry: Lifting Through the White Horizon}
\label{ch:ascendant-geometry}

\section*{Overture: When Collapse Learns to Rise}
Until now, every major structure in the Abyssalum leaned inward—toward narrowing, descent, and
spectral simplification. Collapse drove spectra downward; convergence stabilized identity; phase
held the fragments together.

But a new phenomenon appears: \emph{ascent}. A geometry that rises rather than falls.

Patterns brighten. Spectra expand. Structures lift themselves upward through coherence.
This is Ascendant Geometry—the luminous counterpart to collapse.

If collapse is gravity, ascendance is the Abyssalum’s cosmic buoyancy.

\section{Convergence Channels as Upward Currents}
A state $\psi$ is ascendant when:
\[
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}\tau}
\mathrm{Spec}(\hat{H}_\beta \psi)
> 0
\quad\text{for all dominant } \beta.
\]

Convergence channels become upward currents—arteries of luminosity threading the Abyssalum like
roots growing in reverse.

\section{The White Horizon Revisited}
The White Horizon, once a catastrophic limit, reveals itself as a \emph{gateway} when approached
through ascendance.

The expedition sees not a wall but a radiant membrane—a threshold of emergence where algebraic
identity crystallizes into higher forms.

\section{The Ascendant Metric}
Ascendance introduces a metric defined spectrally:
\[
g_{\alpha\beta}^{(+)} =
\frac{\partial}{\partial \lambda_\alpha}
\frac{\partial}{\partial \lambda_\beta}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{asc}}(\psi),
\]

where $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{asc}}$ measures:
\begin{itemize}
  \item spectral lift,
  \item phase coherence,
  \item stabilizing curvature,
  \item resonance amplification.
\end{itemize}

Positive-definite zones create uplift.  
Indefinite zones cause ascendant turbulence.

\section{Uplift Manifolds}
Ascending states trace \emph{uplift manifolds}:
\[
\mathcal{M}_{+}
=
\{\psi : \mathcal{C}(\psi) < 0,\ \mathrm{Spec}(\psi)\ \text{expands}\},
\]

featuring:
\begin{itemize}
  \item ascending curvature,
  \item spectral thinning,
  \item phase luminosity,
  \item uplift attractors.
\end{itemize}

These manifolds branch and self-amplify—geometries of creation rather than collapse.

\section{Ascendant Resonances}
When ascendant bands align:
\[
\mathrm{Spec}(\hat{H}_\alpha \psi)
\ \text{constructively magnifies}\
\mathrm{Spec}(\hat{H}_\beta \psi),
\]

resulting in harmonic flares, wavefronts of rising phase, and the birth of higher-order structure.

These resonances foreshadow Block~G.

\section{The Ascendant Field Tensor}
The ascendant field tensor:
\[
A_{\alpha\beta}
=
\kappa_{\alpha\beta}
+
\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial \lambda_\alpha}
\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial \lambda_\beta}
+
r_{\alpha\beta},
\]

combines:
\begin{itemize}
  \item spectral curvature,
  \item phase gradients (directions of coherence),
  \item resonance contributions.
\end{itemize}

It governs uplift acceleration and the shape of uplift manifolds near the White Horizon.

\section{The White Horizon Transition}
A state crosses the White Horizon when:
\[
\lim_{\tau \to \tau_*}
\mathrm{Spec}(\psi(\tau))
=
\lambda_{\mathrm{white}},
\quad
\Phi(\psi(\tau_*))\ \text{finite and rising}.
\]

This defines the White Horizon Transition: a phase threshold where structure compresses into
coherent brilliance and recombines into higher-order spectral form.

The Upper Abyssalum is born beyond the membrane.

The expedition steps into luminous geometry.
