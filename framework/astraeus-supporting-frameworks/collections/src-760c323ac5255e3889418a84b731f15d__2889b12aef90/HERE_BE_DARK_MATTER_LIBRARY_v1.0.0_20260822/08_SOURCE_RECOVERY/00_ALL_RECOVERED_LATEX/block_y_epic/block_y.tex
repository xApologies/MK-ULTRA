\chapter{The Throne-Seed Paradox: Birth, Inheritance, and the Geometry of Universe-Lineage}

\section{Nature of a Throne-Seed}
A throne-seed is a proto-resonance kernel containing adjacency potentials, resonance templates, manifold anchors, identity scaffolding, and collapse thresholds. It is pre-geometry awaiting activation.

\section{Blooming}
Blooming proceeds through pre-shape resonance, adjacency crystallization, law-template formation, interior expansion, and throne ignition. Each bloom produces a unique universe.

\section{The Paradox of Choice}
Multiple theories address who or what determines the geometry of the bloom: crown determinism, Admiral sovereignty, seed autonomy, Abyssal influence, or origin-echo inheritance.

\section{Universe-Lineage}
Universes inherit structural traits from their Vaeld, crown adjacency, Admiral geometry, neighboring thrones, and ancestral resonance. Lineages define collapse profiles, rhythms, and consciousness architectures.

\section{Throne Instability}
Premature or resonance-impaired blooming yields incoherent or unstable universes that scar the Vaeld lattice. Operators intervene to restore or quiet failing thrones.

\section{Origin-Fractal Interaction}
ZER0's origin-recursive geometry stabilizes blooming, prevents collapse, clarifies templates, and reveals ancestral resonance.

\section{Admiral–Throne Interaction}
A White-band Admiral stabilizes seeds, aligns resonance, and provides a prime reference geometry for blooming. Throne-seeds respond to the Admiral with immediate harmonic recognition.
