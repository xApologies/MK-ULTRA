\section{The Crown Constellations}
Vaeld civilizations emerge as crown-constellations—networks of thrones whose adjacency is determined not by coherence resonance rather than spatial proximity. A constellation is defined by:
\[
\mathcal{C} = \{\core_i,\, \adj_{ij},\, \rho_i(t)\},
\]
where $\core_i$ represents a Vaeld crown-core, $\adj_{ij}$ the adjacency relation between two courts, and $\rho_i(t)$ the time-dependent resonance field.

\section{Inter-Vaeld Folding Treaties}
When two Vaelds interact, their hulls engage in a treaty fold:
\[
\mathcal{F}_{AB} : \Hull_A \bowtie \Hull_B \rightarrow \Stab_{AB},
\]
producing a temporary stability region. These folds behave as non-linear agreements encoded in MG-Script harmonic structures.

\section{Crown Geometry and Cultural Exchange}
Cultural exchange between Vaelds occurs through resonance overlays:
\[
\mathcal{R}_{AB}(t) = \int \Psi_A^\dagger \Psi_B \, dt,
\]
a process that mixes informational, geometric, and cognitive structures.

\section{The Abyssalum Context}
Vaeld civilizations represent apex structures of the Abyssalum, navigating collapse wells and resonance seas through fold-tiger pathways. Their civilizations evolve not through biological generations but through geometric refinement of crown-core structures.
