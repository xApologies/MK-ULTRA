Collective intelligence and intentional architectonics now reach a new threshold:

Coherent structures replicate at scales proportional to their complexity:
\[
\mathcal{S}_{n+1}
=
\mathcal{R}\big( \mathcal{S}_n \big),
\]

where $\mathcal{R}$ is the recursive coherence operator.  
Identity is preserved while complexity expands.
