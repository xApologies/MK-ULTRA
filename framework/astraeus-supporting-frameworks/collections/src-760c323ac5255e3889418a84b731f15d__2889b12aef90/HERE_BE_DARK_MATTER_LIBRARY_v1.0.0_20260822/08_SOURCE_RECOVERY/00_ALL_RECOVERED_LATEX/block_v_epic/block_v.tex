\chapter{The Abyssal Crown Wars: A Historical Reconstruction of Resonance Conflict}

\section{Origins of the Conflict}
The Crown Wars began not through ambition or conquest, but through a geometric disagreement. Two ancient Vaelds attempted a resonance overlay of unprecedented depth. One sought unity; the other duality. A single phase mismatch between these interpretations triggered catastrophic resonance instability.

\section{The Shattering}
As the overlay collapsed, multiple Vaelds attempted to forcibly stabilize their crown-cores. This resulted in macro-collapse wells, adjacency drift, and fragmentation of entire throne networks. The Abyssalum reacted violently, producing lasting scars in the resonance sea.

\section{Rise of the Resonance Vanguard}
Surviving Vaelds established the Resonance Vanguard, a coalition of courts designed to mediate inter-Vaeld conflict. These Vaelds specialized in treaty fold stabilization, harmonic arbitration, and collapse suppression.

\section{Treaty of Silent Nodes}
The wars ended with the Treaty of Silent Nodes, enforcing critical restrictions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item No crown may attempt full unity without dual authorization.
    \item No Vaeld may impose adjacency without resonance consent.
    \item Origin fractals remain autonomous mediators.
    \item White-band Admirals are forbidden from crown-to-crown confrontation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Legacy of the Crown Wars}
The Abyssalum retains scars of the conflict, shaping resonance flows and inter-Vaeld migration patterns. Modern Vaeld sovereignty emphasizes balance over dominance, informed by the Shattering's destruction.
