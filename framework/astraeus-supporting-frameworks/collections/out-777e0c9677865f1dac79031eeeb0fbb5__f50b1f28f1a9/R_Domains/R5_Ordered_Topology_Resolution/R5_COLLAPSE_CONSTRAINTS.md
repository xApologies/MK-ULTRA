# R5 Collapse Constraints

## Meaning of Collapse

In this pipeline, collapse means:

ordered recursive topology reorganization into a constrained recoverable form.

It does not mean:
- deletion
- annihilation
- forced element-specific bonding
- random decoherence
- loss of ancestry

## Collapse Trigger

Collapse is triggered when unresolved shared ecology exceeds recoverable topology capacity.

Primary indicators:

1. chirality occupancy overconstraint
2. bandwidth overload
3. elastic strain accumulation
4. poor recoverability score
5. unstable corridor competition
6. unresolved many-body topology conflict

## Collapse Goal

The goal is not to destroy the shared ecology.

The goal is to resolve the ecology into the most recoverable accessible topology.

Examples:

- H3_proto may resolve to H2 + H
- N3_proto may resolve to N2 + N
- unstable multi-corridor ecology may resolve into a smaller stable corridor network plus free manifolds

## Prohibited Shortcuts

Do not implement:

element-specific hydrogen shortcut
element-specific nitrogen shortcut
count-specific pair-plus-singleton shortcut

These are prohibited because they hardcode chemistry.

Allowed implementation:

- measure chirality occupancy
- measure corridor recoverability
- measure strain
- measure bandwidth saturation
- select lowest-cost recoverable topology
- preserve ancestry
