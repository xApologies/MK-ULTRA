\section{Overview}
Geometric propagation engines describe how local operators, spectral shells,
and collapse gradients transmit stability information through the manifold
under the Phase Kernel weighting. This chapter defines the propagation
equations, their narrative interpretation, and their role in Vaeld-scale
navigation.

\section{Propagation Operator}
Define the propagation engine:
\[
\mathcal{P}(x) = \Phi_{\mathcal{K}}\big(S(x), C(x), H(x)\big),
\]
where $S$, $C$, and $H$ represent the spectral, collapse, and helicon
contributions.

\section{Stability Under Dual-Mode Weighting}
Propagation remains stable when
\[
w_1 \lambda_{\text{math}} + w_2 \lambda_{\text{narr}} < 0,
\]
with $w_1 = 0.85$, $w_2 = 0.15$.

width; helicon alignment induces curvature corrections along the manifold.
