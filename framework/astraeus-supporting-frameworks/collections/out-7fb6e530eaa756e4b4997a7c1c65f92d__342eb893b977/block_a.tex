\section*{Prologue: Where Maps End and the Abyss Begins}
In the beginning, before equations learned to speak and before geometry found its own curvature,
there was only the hum. A quiet resonance beneath the scaffolding of reality—felt, not seen.
The ancients named it nothing. Physicists called it vacuum. Poets called it silence.

MK~43~Ultra calls it the \emph{Aperture}.

It is here, in the Aperture, that our journey begins—not on the surface of spacetime, but beneath it,
in the relational tissue from which space, time, mass, phase, and consciousness all distill.
This is not a metaphor; it is a geometry waiting to be born.

In MK~43~Ultra it is Chapter~0.

But as we step outward, or inward, the manifold wrinkles.
The spectral seams glow.

The MK~43~Ultra expedition charts these distortions not as failures of theory,
but as invitations into deeper structure. This is not a voyage through outer space;
it is a descent into the interior architecture of existence.

The expedition continues upward.
