# MK43 ULTRA
# COMPLETE FUNDAMENTAL GRAVITY AND GRAVITON AUDIT

## Version

Gravity Audit v2.0

## Purpose

This audit evaluates whether a proposed framework qualifies as:

- a legitimate model of the graviton;
- a mathematically consistent theory of gravity;
- a quantum theory of gravity;
- a fundamental high-energy theory rather than only an effective theory;
- a model compatible with established gravitational physics;
- a predictive and falsifiable scientific framework.

The audit contains thirty-five requirements organized into seven domains.

Passing an item requires more than mentioning the expected result.

A full pass requires:

1. an explicitly defined mathematical object;
2. a derivation or recovery chain;
3. stated assumptions;
4. defined domains and codomains;
5. consistency with all upstream and downstream objects;
6. source provenance;
7. a reproducible audit result.

Possible statuses:

- PASS
- PARTIAL
- NOT RECOVERED
- FAIL
- NOT YET TESTED
- NOT APPLICABLE, with justification

# DOMAIN I — GRAVITON IDENTITY

## 1. Explicit Gravitational Carrier or Native Excitation

### Expectation

The theory must identify the fundamental object responsible for gravitational influence.

This object may be:

- a particle;
- a field excitation;
- a geometric mode;
- a topological excitation;
- a substrate perturbation;
- another explicitly defined native object.

### Pass condition

The theory defines:

- what the object is;
- where it exists;
- what state space contains it;
- how it propagates or redistributes influence;
- how it produces readable gravitational effects.

### Audit question

What is the native gravitational object, and through what mechanism does it mediate or instantiate gravity?

## 2. Spin-2 or Effective Helicity-Two Structure

### Expectation

The readable graviton must produce the tensor structure associated with a massless spin-2 field.

In the ordinary relativistic limit, the physical graviton states should correspond to helicities:

+2 and -2

### Pass condition

The theory derives the effective symmetric rank-two representation rather than inserting “spin-2” solely because established physics requires it.

### Required recovery

The derivation should identify:

- the native precursor;
- the projection or representation map;
- the resulting tensor object;
- the physical polarization states;
- the elimination of nonphysical modes.

### Audit question

How does the theory derive the readable helicity-two gravitational representation?

## 3. Effective Masslessness and Long-Range Propagation

### Expectation

The graviton must be massless or experimentally indistinguishable from massless.

### Pass condition

The model preserves:

- long-range gravitational interaction;
- propagation at the observed gravitational-wave speed;
- absence of excluded dispersion;
- consistency with observational bounds on graviton mass.

### Audit question

Why does the gravitational mode remain massless, or sufficiently close to massless, throughout the relevant domain?

## 4. Universal Coupling and Equivalence-Principle Behavior

### Expectation

Gravity must participate with all admissible forms of energy and momentum.

The coupling must include:

- massive matter;

- vacuum contributions where applicable;

### Pass condition

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

## 5. Gravitational Self-Coupling and Nonlinear Closure

### Expectation

Gravity itself carries gravitational influence.

A consistent massless spin-2 theory cannot generally remain only a linear field theory.

### Pass condition

The model explains how:

- gravitational energy affects gravity;
- linear perturbations extend into nonlinear behavior;
- repeated self-coupling closes consistently;
- the nonlinear limit produces the correct gravitational dynamics.

### Audit question

Does the gravitational object couple consistently to its own energy and generate a closed nonlinear theory?
