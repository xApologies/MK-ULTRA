## 4. Forward and backward Resolution motion

The source has two different status levels.

### Source-stable direction: fine -> coarse

For `R_i <= R_j`:

`q_ji : B_H,Rj -> B_H,Ri`

forgets distinctions unavailable at the coarser Resolution.

No global inverse is assumed.

### Source live-model candidate: coarse -> fine

When ancestry is retained, the live model defines a refinement morphism:

`rho_ij : F_Ri(U_H) -> F_Rj(U_H)`.

It must reveal inherited organization rather than invent it.

The paired target is:

`q_ji o rho_ij ~= id`

at recoverable/admissible-equivalence level.

Thus "backwards and forwards" is mathematically meaningful as:

`refine <-> coarsen`

but refinement is not obtained by inverting the coarse image alone.

It re-accesses the persistent parent Hydrogen organization.

## 5. The two domains move oppositely

This is the important geometric consequence.

Under monotone refinement:

`F_Ri(U_H) subseteq F_Rj(U_H)`.

The **readable section space expands** because more inherited organization becomes
distinguishable.

But the conflation fiber:

`Dom_R^H([H]_R) = q_R^-1([H]_R)`

generally **contracts**, because fewer formally distinct states remain identified as one
readable class.

Under coarsening, the opposite occurs:
- readable distinctions collapse;
- the readout-preimage/conflation domain expands.

So a fixed Hydrogen anchor carries a two-sided Resolution geometry.
