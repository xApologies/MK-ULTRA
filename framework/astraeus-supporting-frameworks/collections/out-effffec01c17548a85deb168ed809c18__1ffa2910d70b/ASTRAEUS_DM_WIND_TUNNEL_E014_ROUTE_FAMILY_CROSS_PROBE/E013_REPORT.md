## Result

E012 showed that **224/296** representative robust states can preserve at least one
connected two-corridor route across T5 under every tested condition, but **0/296**
retain one single fixed route under every condition.

That suggests the persistent object is not one immutable road.

=

`F_route(x) = union_theta Gamma_theta(x)`.

This is the state's **persistent route family** on the Rainbow Bus.

## Interpretation

This gives Rainbow Road a sharper role in the dark-matter sandbox.

Pipeline/ER Foam supplies and resolves local relations.

Rainbow Bus supplies the available route fabric.

The stable hidden object can be represented by the **family of persistent routes
available to the state**, not one globally frozen path.

Thus two states can satisfy:

`M_R(x) = M_R(y)`

while also satisfying:

`F_route(x) != F_route(y)`.

That is a distributed routing distinction invisible to the coarse readout.
