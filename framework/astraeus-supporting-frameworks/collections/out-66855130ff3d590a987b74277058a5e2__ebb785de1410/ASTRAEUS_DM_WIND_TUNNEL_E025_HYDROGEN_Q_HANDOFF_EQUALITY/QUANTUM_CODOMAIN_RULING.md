# E025 — Quantum Codomain Ruling

The source intentionally makes atomic reverse recovery quotient-valued:

`R_H : X_H -> Q_H / ~_H`.

The Hydrogen Anchor Corollary closes as:

`R_H o A_H(Q_H) = [Q_H]`.

Therefore a comparison of two environment-conditioned Hydrogen states should not
demand equality of gauge, phase, or representation coordinates that the Atomic
Profile explicitly does not retain.

For the Hydrogen anchor, define the source-backed local restriction:

`rho_Q,H^anchor := R_H o pr_H`

where `pr_H : Z_H -> X_H` is the canonical factor projection from

`Z_H = ENV_ER fiber_product_T X_H`

to its preserved Hydrogen atomic-carrier factor.

For the current fresh/conditioned pair, both runs use the same mounted `X_H`
source factor and the bridge guards preserve that atomic carrier identity.

Hence:

`rho_Q,H^anchor(F_fresh) = rho_Q,H^anchor(F_conditioned) = [Q_H]`.

This closes the Hydrogen admissible-equivalence quantum side.

It does NOT silently complete the Version-1 global map:

`rho_Q : F -> Q_QFT`.

The remaining quantum formalism is to state whether global `Q_QFT` is itself
quotient-valued on the Hydrogen anchor, or whether raw Q and quotient-Q are kept
as two explicitly related levels.
