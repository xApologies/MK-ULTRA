## 3. Factorization criterion

Let

```text
rho_E : E_U -> R
```

be a scalar native-load readout on admissible UCM source/event states, and let

```text
P_T : E_U -> T_read
```

be the still-open readable stress-energy projection.

A scalar-only stress-energy law

```text
P_T = F o rho_E
```

exists **if and only if** `P_T` is constant on every `rho_E` fiber:

```text
rho_E(e1)=rho_E(e2)
=>
P_T(e1)=P_T(e2).
```

This is the ordinary quotient-factorization condition.

## 4. V31-T3 — Scalar shortcut no-go

No recovered source proves that `P_T` is constant on `rho_E` fibers.
