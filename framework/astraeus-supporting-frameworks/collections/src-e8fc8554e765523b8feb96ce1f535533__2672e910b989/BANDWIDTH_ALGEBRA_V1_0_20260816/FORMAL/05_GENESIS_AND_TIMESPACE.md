# 5. Genesis Field and Time-Space Integration

## Current Atlas ordering

The live Atlas currently uses the following top-level dependency order:

```text
Genesis Field
 -> Time-Space
 -> Resolution
 -> Bandwidth
 -> Chirality
 -> Persistence
```

This is a current Atlas ruling. Earlier foundation papers preserve historical orderings and are retained in the source ledger.

## Genesis Field source equation

Historical master form:

```text
G = G * SUM[d=0..INF](R_d * B_d).
```

Source meanings:

- `G`: recursively self-expressing Genesis Field;
- `R_d`: distinguishable organization at depth `d`;
- `B_d`: available organizational freedom at depth `d`.

## Typed lift

In Bandwidth Algebra v1.0, lift the scalar `B_d` to the formal object:

```text
A_B,d
```

and treat scalar `B_d` as a declared valuation/readout:

```text
B_d = beta_d(A_B,d).
```

This avoids forcing the master Genesis equation itself to carry the full operator algebra.

## Time-Space placement

Time-Space supplies the typed organizational domain on which Resolution and Bandwidth are instantiated. A local `S^2`, an `S^3` continuity domain, or complete `T^5` can therefore carry different Bandwidth substructures while remaining descendants of one Genesis-field organization.
