\section{Spectral Stress Functional}
Spectral integrity is measured by the stress functional:
\[
\mathcal{S}(x) = 
w_1 \left| \nabla S(x) \right|
+ 
w_2\,\Theta_{\mathrm{spectral}}(x),
\]
where $S(x)$ is the spectral field and $\Theta_{\mathrm{spectral}}$ encodes
narrative resonance effects under the Phase Kernel.

\section{Integrity Condition}
Spectral integrity holds when:
\[
\mathcal{S}(x) < \sigma_{\mathrm{crit}},
\]
with $\sigma_{\mathrm{crit}}$ representing the stress threshold determined by 
the manifold’s triadic balance.

\section{Interpretation}
Under stress, spectral pathways narrow and intensify. Collapse gradients apply
counter-pressure, and helicon curvature distributes strain through rotational
shear. When properly aligned, these forces preserve coherence even under 
extreme spectral compression.
