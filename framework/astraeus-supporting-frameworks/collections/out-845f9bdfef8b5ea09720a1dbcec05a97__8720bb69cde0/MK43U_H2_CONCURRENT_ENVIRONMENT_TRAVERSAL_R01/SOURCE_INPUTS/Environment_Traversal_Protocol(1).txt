
MK43 Ultra Environment Traversal Protocol

Purpose:
Prevent fallback to conceptual chemistry or simulator assumptions.

Core Rule:
The manifold is the runtime/state.
The environment is the wind tunnel.
The environment is moved around the manifold.

Do NOT ask:
"What molecule should chemistry produce?"

Instead ask:
"What persistent topology survives T0->R7 traversal?"

Workflow:

1. Load manifold(s).
2. Insert manifold(s) into environment.
3. Move through:
   T0 T1 T2 T3 T4 T5 T6 T7
   R0 R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7
4. Observe:
   - chirality
   - corridor formation
   - closure behavior
   - persistence retention
   - topology redistribution
5. Report R7 topology.
6. Preserve manifold inventory.
7. If stable, save resulting topology as a molecular manifold.

Important:
Do not use conceptual pairing logic.
Do not use standard chemistry prediction.
Do not assume executable simulation is required.

Nitrogen example:
10 N -> 5 N2

Persistence audit:
2 N2 -> 2 N2

Oxygen example:
7 O -> O3 + O2 + O2

The environment is treated as a mathematical traversal framework.
The output is the recovered persistent topology at R7.
