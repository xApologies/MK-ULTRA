\begin{abstract}
We present a unified theoretical framework that connects Planck-scale structure,
quantum gravity, emergent spacetime, transverse-traceless graviton propagation,
entanglement-induced topology change, and cosmological evolution. The model is 
built from a discrete network of Planck-scale beads forming a dynamic time-shell

dynamics, ER bridge formation, and classical general relativity in the continuum 
limit. Detailed appendices provide full derivations of the TT kernel, bead 
Hamiltonian, continuum limit, ER-bridge geometry, and cosmological behavior.
