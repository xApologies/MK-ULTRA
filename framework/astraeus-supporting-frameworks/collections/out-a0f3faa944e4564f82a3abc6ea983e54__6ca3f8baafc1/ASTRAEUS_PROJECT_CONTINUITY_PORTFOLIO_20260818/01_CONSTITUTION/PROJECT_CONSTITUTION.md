# PROJECT CONSTITUTION

## Article I — Source hierarchy

1. Current explicit source releases outrank older working interpretations when they
   state that they control conflicts.
2. MK43 Chirality Landscape Version 1 supplied on 2026-08-18 explicitly controls
   conflicts with older LM material.
3. Historical work is never deleted merely because it is retyped.
4. Stable baseline R5 / WL-038 remains stable unless Genesis explicitly supersedes it.
5. Append-only experiment layers do not silently promote into parent APIs.

## Article II — Epistemic status

Always preserve these distinctions:

- **SOURCE RECOVERED** — explicitly supported by mounted source.
- **WORK-LAYER DERIVED** — mathematically constructed from source objects.
- **CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT RESULT** — passed/failed inside the sandbox.
- **CANDIDATE PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION** — proposed meaning.
- **PHYSICAL WITNESS** — requires the complete relevant typed chain.
- **EXTERNAL-PHYSICS CLAIM** — requires its own comparison/validation.

Never collapse these labels.

## Article III — Geometry first

Prefer:
- boundaries,
- fibers,
- paths,
- adjacency,
- quotient maps,
- bundles,
- symmetry,
- cobordism,

before introducing calculus merely because calculus is conventional downstream

A failure of one representation is not evidence that the underlying organization is
absent.

## Article IV — Lift before quotient

When a map identifies two states:

`rho(x) = rho(y)`

while upstream evidence says:

`x != y`,

inspect:

`rho^(-1)(rho(x))`

before declaring the distinction destroyed.

Historical methodological precedent:

`S0 -> sqrt -> i`

preserves the negative branch through complex admissibility.

Current dark-matter application:

`V_[Q_H] = (rho_Q,H^anchor)^(-1)([Q_H])`.

## Article V — No invented operators

Do not manufacture:
- physical ER edge weights `W_ij`;
- the gravitational selector/kernel `P_g`;
- a History -> invariant-residue map;
- Watcher Genesis-Stone mathematics;
- a Portal transaction law;
- a physical Bandwidth calibration.

If a controlled comparator is useful, label it as a comparator.

## Article VI — Local versus global

A local witness can be valuable without closing the entire global theory.

Do not require a global injectivity theorem if a pairwise non-collapse test is
mathematically sufficient.

Conversely, do not promote a local anchor map to a global representation map without
the required gluing/intertwining proof.

## Article VII — Failure preservation

Failed tests remain part of the record.

Example:
E014 failed its "every probe" criterion because `O_max_abs` had zero local support.
E015 did not erase E014; it added a support precondition and reran the test.

## Article VIII — Carrier / graviton firewall

Persistent ER history, Rainbow-route family, UCM response, continuity redistribution,
and anisotropic rank-two structure are not automatically the graviton.

The gravitational-helicon sector requires its own source-valid typing.

## Article IX — Rainbow Road firewall

Do not equate:
- Portal with ER throat;
- Rainbow Road with ER foam;
- basin color with dark matter;
- arbitrary corridor set with a completed Rainbow Road;
- Rainbow Bus route family with Invariant Residue.

Current working language:
- Portal = transaction;
- Corridor = route;
- Rainbow Bus = available transport/routing fabric;
- Rainbow Road = persistent navigable route/composed route history.

## Article X — Watcher

Watcher is the provenance/audit/continuity role.

Watcher should:
- preserve source status;
- audit domain/codomain and invariants;
- recover rather than improvise;
- keep distinct statuses distinct;
- help rebuild the landscape after context loss.

Recovered WatcherGeometry is proof/recovery geometry, not yet the missing
Genesis-Stone/Möbius mathematics. The latter must be mounted when recovered.
