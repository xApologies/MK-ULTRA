# WHY THIS PROJECT

The dark-matter branch asks whether a representation can erase a distinction from one
readout while another lawful representation still responds to it.

The central pattern is:

`same readable identity`
+
`different retained organization`
+
`different response`.

This is not treated as a license to call every hidden variable "dark matter."

The research program tries to close, in order:

1. existence of readout-degenerate internal states;
2. survival of their distinction under admissible Pipeline/ER evolution;
3. endogenous generation rather than hand-authored differences;
4. cross-probe visibility;
5. universal-coupling response;
6. conservation;
7. tensor/rank-two eligibility;
8. quantum-side equality under a source-defined handoff;
9. gravitational-side non-collapse under the source-defined bridge.

Only after the full typed chain closes can the branch claim a canonical
representation-level dark witness.

The most important conceptual shift to date is that hidden organization is increasingly
described as **History / relational routing / vertical fiber structure**, not as an
extra pile of invisible local matter inserted by hand.
