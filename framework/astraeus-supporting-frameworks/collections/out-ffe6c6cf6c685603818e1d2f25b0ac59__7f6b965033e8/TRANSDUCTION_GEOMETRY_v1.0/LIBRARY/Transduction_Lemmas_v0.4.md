# TRANSDUCTION LEMMAS v0.1

## L0 — Identity Lift Nonemptiness
A1 + D1 imply x in LiftSet(id_U,x), hence the identity lift family is nonempty.

## L1 — Sequential Lift Inclusion
D1 + A2 + D1 imply that sequentially admissible lifts are admissible for the composite dependency arrow.

## L2 — Connection Cannot Select Outside Structural Admissibility
D5 + D2 imply Conn_B(f,x) belongs to LiftSet(f,x) whenever selection is defined.

## L3 — Operative Multiplicity Cannot Exceed Formal Multiplicity
D2 implies Bandwidth may remove available branches but cannot create formally inadmissible branches.

## L4 — Resolution Can Collapse Distinct Formal Branches
D3 + D4 imply distinct formal lifts can belong to the same readable Resolution class.


## L5 — Transition Preservation of Lift Families
D1 + A3 imply declared transition maps carry admissible lift members to admissible lift members in the transitioned presentation.

## L6 — Transitioned Connection Selection Remains Structurally Admissible
D2 + D5 + L5 imply a transitioned connection-selected state remains inside the transitioned formal admissible lift family. This does not prove connection equivariance.


## L7 — Vanishing Descent Defect Class
A4 implies [D_ijk]_~D = [id]_~D on every valid triple overlap.

## L8 — Triple-Overlap Coherence
D20 transition compatibility plus A4 implies that triple transition around i -> j -> k -> i returns the local family to an equivalent presentation.

## L9 — Transition Compatibility Implies Descent Compatibility under A4
D20 + A4 imply D21 on valid triple overlaps.


## L10 — Cocycle Consistency of Gluing Identifications
A4 plus descent compatibility imply that transition-generated identifications are consistent on valid triple overlaps modulo Descent Equivalence.

## L11 — Quotient Candidate Carries Local Images
D31 provides canonical local maps into the quotient candidate.

## L12 — Recovery Compatibility Produces Local Realization
D32 guarantees that the quotient candidate recovers each local component up to the declared local/descent equivalence.

## L13 — Witness Closure Produces a Valid Gluing Witness
D33 converts a qualifying quotient/recovery candidate into a valid Gluing Witness.
