# 09 — Energy as Transduction Invariant

The Atlas inherits the active energy definition from the chirality-landscape live model.

## Definition

Energy is treated as a cross-representational balance invariant: the conserved accounting content that survives an admissible transduction even when its carrier/representation changes.

Schematic:

```text
carrier_A --T--> carrier_B
```

while the energy balance commutes through the transduction.

For an open local domain `D`:

```text
dE_D/dt = P_in - P_out + P_source.
```

This permits local storage and delayed release without requiring instantaneous input/output equality.

## Equivalence-class formulation

```text
(E_a,x) ~_T (E_b,y)
```

when `y=T_ab(x)` and the required energetic balance is preserved.

Then:

```text
mathcal_E[x] := [(E_a,x)]_~T.
```

## Atlas mapping target

Energy should be tracked:

```text
inside S2 local transductions
and
across S3 horizontal chirality transport.
```

A future version should connect this invariant explicitly to QFT Hamiltonian/spectral representations and GR stress-energy/conservation representations without silently identifying them.
