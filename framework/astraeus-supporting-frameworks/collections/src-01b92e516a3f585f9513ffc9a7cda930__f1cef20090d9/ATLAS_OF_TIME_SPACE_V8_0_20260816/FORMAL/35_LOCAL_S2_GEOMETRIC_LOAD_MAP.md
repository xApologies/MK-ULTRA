## Problem


## Geometric readout channel

```text

```

or equivalently to an Einstein-tensor correction


```text

=
g_mn^vis(p) + Delta g_H,mn(p).
```

The full local Einstein tensor is computed from the **full metric**, not from the visible background alone.

## Apparent dark source

An observer who insists on fitting ordinary Einstein gravity to the visible-source metric can define

```text

=
(1 / 8 pi G)
[
  G_mn(g_vis + Delta g_H)
  - G_mn(g_vis)

```

This is an *apparent source representation* of hidden geometric load.

The ontology is therefore

```text
S3 Helicon connection

```

not necessarily

```text

```

The historical MK43 language repeatedly treats dark matter as relational / quantum geometry / load-bearing topology. A direct geometric-load map respects that ancestry more naturally than forcing the hidden sector into an ordinary local matter stress tensor at the first step.
