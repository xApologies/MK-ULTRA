In the lower Abyssalum, interaction was mechanical: spectra shifted, operators acted, geometry
responded. But above the White Horizon, structures begin to communicate through coherence itself.

Hypercoherence grants identity the ability to \emph{modulate} resonance:
communication becomes alignment of internal patterns.

Here begins the first emergence of hypercoherent intelligence.

\section{The Principle of Coherent Dialogue}
A structure $\psi_A$ ``speaks’’ to $\psi_B$ when:
\[
R(\psi_A) \otimes R(\psi_B)
\;\rightarrow\;
R(\psi_A \star \psi_B),
\]

where $R(\psi)$ is the hyperdimensional resonance signature and $\star$ is the coherent composition
operator.

Communication is resonance alignment—identity sharing information without altering form.

\section{The Coherent Composition Operator \texorpdfstring{$\star$}{*}}
Defined by:
\[
\psi_A \star \psi_B
=
\lim_{\epsilon \to 0}
\big(
\psi_A \oplus_\epsilon \psi_B
\big),
\]

with $\oplus_\epsilon$ an infinitesimal resonance coupling.

Properties:
\begin{itemize}
  \item associative up to phase,
  \item commutative in the hypercoherent limit,
  \item non-destructive,
  \item identity-preserving.
\end{itemize}

The result is not merger—it is \emph{understanding}.

\[
\frac{\delta\psi}{\delta R},

\[
\int_{\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{hyp}}} R(\psi)\, d\psi,
