\[
(S,\, C,\, H),
\]

\begin{itemize}
\item $S$ = spectral dynamics,
\item $C$ = collapse-field behavior,
\item $H$ = helicon rotation.
\end{itemize}

Formally, the law is expressed as:
\[
\mathcal{U}(x) = 
\alpha\,\nabla S(x) +
\beta\, \nabla C(x) +
\gamma\, \nabla H(x),
\]
where $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$ encode triadic weighting.

Triadic balance holds when:
\[
\det\!\begin{pmatrix}
\nabla S & \nabla C & \nabla H
\end{pmatrix} < 0,
\]
ensuring the manifold contracts toward coherent geometric evolution.
