\section{Neural Phase Field Definition}
Define a neural phase:
\[
\tau_{\mathrm{neural}}(x,t) = \sum_i a_i \Psi_i(x,t),

where \(\Psi_i\) are neural activation modes.

\section{Behavioral Encoding Law}
Behavior emerges via:
\[
B(t) = \int f(\partial\tau_{\mathrm{neural}})\, dV.
\]
