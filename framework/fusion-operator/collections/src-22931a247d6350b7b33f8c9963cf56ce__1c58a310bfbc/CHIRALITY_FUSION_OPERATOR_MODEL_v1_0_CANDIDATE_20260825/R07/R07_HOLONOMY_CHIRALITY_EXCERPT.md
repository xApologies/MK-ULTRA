## Source-supported constraints used by R07

From `02_CHIRALITY_FABRIC.md`:

> Chirality = organizational / admissibility grammar.

The source states that handedness is one readable expression of chirality, and that at primitive seed level **arrangement, not population count alone**, carries the distinction.

It also states:

> Local chirality states may vary while belonging to one global chirality fabric.

And its chirality-ledger firewall states that domains may read/respond to chirality but may not invent or erase chirality ancestry.

From `08_CHIRALITY_TRANSPORT_AND_HOLONOMY.md`:

The source defines chirality transport as an admissible inherited realization and explicitly rejects independently reassigning a chirality label during transport.

## R07 use

R07 uses these as constraints on what a conservation statement may mean. It does **not** use them to assume the new Fusion quotient already has a scalar chirality observable or a physical conservation law.
