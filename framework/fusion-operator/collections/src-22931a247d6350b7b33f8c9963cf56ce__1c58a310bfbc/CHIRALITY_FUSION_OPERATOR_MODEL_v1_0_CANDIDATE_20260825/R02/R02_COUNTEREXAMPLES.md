# R02 — Counterexample Ledger

## CE-S01 — Connected does not mean source-erased

The R01 quotient Q is connected through q, but the canonical source maps are injective.

Therefore:

\[
\text{connected global carrier}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{source images destroyed}.
\]

This blocks the shortcut "one structure, therefore no A/B distinction remains."

---

## CE-S02 — Recoverable source images do not imply independent factors

A and B embed into Q, but

\[
A_F\cap B_F=\{q\}\neq\varnothing.
\]

Thus:

\[
\text{source recoverability}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{free separability}.
\]

---

## CE-S03 — Local inverse is not a global projection

Because j_A is injective, \(j_A^{-1}\) exists on \(A_F\). But this does not specify where \(B_F\setminus\{q\}\) should map under a total \(Q\to A\) retraction.

Thus:

\[
\text{inverse on source image}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{canonical total projection}.
\]

---

## CE-S04 — Tensor language is a type error at R02

R01 defines a finite labeled relational quotient carrier, not Hilbert spaces. Therefore writing

\[
Q=\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B
\]

as an R02 conclusion would change types without a lift.

A future Hilbert lift may be studied, but it cannot retroactively prove R02.

---

## CE-S05 — Bare carrier symmetry loses ordered provenance

The symmetric Fusion mode satisfies a source-swap carrier isomorphism:

\[
Q_\rho(A,B)\cong Q_{\rho^{op}}(B,A).
\]

Therefore the undecorated carrier cannot by itself distinguish the ordered pair (A,B) from (B,A).

The tagged Fusion witness can.

---

## CE-S06 — Readout erasure is not formal subsystem erasure

A Resolution quotient may hide the boundary between \(A_F\) and \(B_F\). This does not prove that the formal source images ceased to exist.

Thus:

\[
\text{observational non-differentiation}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{formal factor erasure}.
\]

---

## CE-S07 — Strong Fusion requires a stronger equivalence relation

If a later operator identifies additional non-interface loci or quotients away source provenance, the canonical maps j_A/j_B may cease to be injective or uniquely recoverable.

That would be a genuinely stronger operator.

It is not the R01 operator and cannot be silently substituted for it.
