# GENESIS COMPOUND GENERATION SPACE

The source Root Generation Law permits future words built from Root+Root or Root+Root+Root. THE TOME therefore exhausts the **syntactic candidate space** over the 20 recovered root concepts without claiming that every combination is a valid word.

- two-root candidates: 400
- three-root candidates: 8000
- total candidates: 8400

Every entry is stored in `MACHINE/candidate_compounds.json` with status `UNATTESTED_SYNTACTIC_CANDIDATE`.

This is intentionally not an auto-generated lexicon. Semantic validity still requires root-family compatibility, grammar, source/curatorial acceptance, scope, and a mathematical typing witness.

## Why this matters

Exhausting the candidate combinatorics lets Genesis search systematically rather than inventing words opportunistically. It also provides a finite v0.1 search universe for automated contradiction, collision, and ancestry tests.
