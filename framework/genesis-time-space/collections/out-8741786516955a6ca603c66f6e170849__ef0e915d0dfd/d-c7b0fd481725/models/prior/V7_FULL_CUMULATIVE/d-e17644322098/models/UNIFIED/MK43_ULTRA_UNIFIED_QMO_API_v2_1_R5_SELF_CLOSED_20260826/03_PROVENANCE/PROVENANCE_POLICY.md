# Provenance policy

- Parent files are read only.
- Every mounted file occurrence is SHA-256 hashed and tied to its top-level source receipt.
- Structured object/equation/edge imports preserve their source namespace and source path.
- Exact duplicate files may share one content row while retaining distinct file-occurrence provenance nodes.
- API-generated source-root addresses and mount edges are marked metadata/navigation; they are not source-derived mathematical claims.
- Same canonical handle across namespaces is recorded separately from mathematical identity/equivalence.
