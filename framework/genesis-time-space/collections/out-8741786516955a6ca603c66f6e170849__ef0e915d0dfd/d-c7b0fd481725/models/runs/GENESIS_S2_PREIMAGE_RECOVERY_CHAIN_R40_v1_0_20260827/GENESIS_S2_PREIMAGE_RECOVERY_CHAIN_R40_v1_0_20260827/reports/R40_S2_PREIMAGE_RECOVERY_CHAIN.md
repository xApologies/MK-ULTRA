# R40 — S2 PREIMAGE RECOVERY-CHAIN SPECIALIZATION

## Verdict

The old Rainbow Road recovery chain specializes cleanly to the new S2 projection-preimage problem.

This produces one real closure and one precise fork.

### Closure 1 — the recovery problem has the same mathematical type

For a fixed localized S2 address p, let F_p be the recovery-relevant decorated subset of Π^{-1}(p).

The preimage behaves exactly like the older non-injective Resolution handoff fiber: several fine persistent-boundary representatives may project to the same coarse/localized address.

Therefore the existing witness-fiber separation theorem applies by specialization:

**A retained witness W uniquely selects a fine representative of F_p iff W|_{F_p} separates every pair of distinct recovery-relevant lineages.**

This is a type-level theorem; it does not require inventing the members of F_p.

### Closure 2 — full History is a candidate discriminator, not an automatic solution

The existing source architecture already uses History/provenance as retained lineage data.

But full History is sufficient on F_p only if distinct recovery-relevant representatives actually have distinct History traces.

So we cannot say “History solves the preimage” merely because History exists.

We must test injectivity/separation on the fiber.

### Closure 3 — the minimal-signature question carries over unchanged

Rainbow Road v0.8 already terminates its recovery chain at:

`@recovery_history_signature_minimality`

which asks whether full ordered History can be replaced by a smaller History/holonomy signature that remains separating on every recovery-relevant fiber and stable under admissible composition.

The S2 preimage problem is a direct new instance of that frontier.

Thus the new QMO is not a new ad hoc selector. It is a specialization:

`@r40/holonomy_signature_candidate`.

### Exact fork

The chain does **not** stop at the abstract recovery discriminator anymore.

That part closes by specialization.

It stops later at two linked unknowns:

1. **Concrete fiber discrimination:** for a specific p, what distinctions among representatives of F_p must the witness retain?
2. **Minimal compositional signature / connection:** can a History-holonomy signature separate those lineages while remaining stable under composition, and does that supply enough decorated structure for a unique connection instance?

The current source defines the type of all of these objects but does not provide the concrete p-specific population or the final chirality connection.
