# 50 — ACTUALIZATION, SYMMETRY, AND NON-FABRICATION

R27–R32 isolate the actualization problem after the persistence machinery closes structurally.

## Viability/Bandwidth circularity firewall

A nontrivial T5 viability kernel depends on the operative transformation graph, which depends on Bandwidth.

Therefore an arbitrarily chosen Bandwidth restriction cannot be used to construct a preferred closure and then cited as proof that the same Bandwidth restriction was forced.

## Symmetry obstruction

If admitted laws are equivariant under a symmetry exchanging two candidates, no invariant rule built solely from those same laws can distinguish the candidates.

## Moduli result

R29 shows that after quotienting obvious presentation symmetries, genuinely inequivalent local candidate classes can remain.

The remaining fork is not a labeling artifact.

## A19 non-fabrication gate

A19 is retained as a candidate non-fabrication rule:

- unique witnessed class may complete;
- unresolved multiplicity remains unresolved;
- absence of a witness does not license invention.

In isolation, A19 + local-only History-conditioned Bandwidth does not autonomously break a genuine fork.

## Coupled-context result

R32 shows that surrounding realized context can change local admissibility and thereby discriminate some candidate classes.

This is an existence witness, not yet the universal update law.
