# 44 — GENESIS RANK AND THE SU(2)/SU(3) NO-GO

R8–R11 repeatedly test whether the persistent chirality mechanism uniquely determines a conventional group.

It does not.

## Definition 44.1 — Recoverable generator rank

For a finite generated persistent scope \(P\), define a minimal recoverable generator rank

\[
\operatorname{mgrank}(P)
\]

as the least number of recoverably independent generators required by the declared witness/invariant contract.

The source develops this as a usable rank notion without assuming a full exchange theorem.

## History independence criterion

History is constitutive but not automatically an independent coordinate.

A History component contributes independent information only when removing/identifying it destroys a distinction required by the recovery/invariant contract.

## Rank-change trichotomy

Persistence can preserve, reduce, or reorganize effective rank. It does not automatically increase rank.

## No-go 44.2

The following do not prove \(SU(3)\):

- six visible basin colors;
- three-body examples;
- an \(S^3\)-class corridor;
- downstream notation containing `SU(3)`.

The following do not prove \(SU(2)\):

- a \(2\times2\) carrier representation;
- a Hopf-compatible representation;
- an \(S^3\) envelope.

A unique group identification requires its own rank, form-preservation, determinant/orientation, and transformation-completeness witnesses.
