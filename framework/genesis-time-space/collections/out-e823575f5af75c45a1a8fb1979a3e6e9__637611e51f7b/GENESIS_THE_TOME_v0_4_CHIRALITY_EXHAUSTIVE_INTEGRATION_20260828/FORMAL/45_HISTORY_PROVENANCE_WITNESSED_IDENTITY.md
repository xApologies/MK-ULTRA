# 45 — HISTORY, PROVENANCE, AND WITNESSED IDENTITY

R11 makes History part of the formal object while rejecting the idea that every History token is a new independent dimension.

## Definition 45.1 — Witnessed object

\[
X^w=(X,H_X,W_X,I_X),
\]

with carrier \(X\), ordered History \(H_X\), provenance/recovery witness \(W_X\), and invariant contract \(I_X\).

## Constitutive-History principle

Equal readout does not imply equal History.

Different parent lineages may converge to one present readout without retroactively becoming the same parent identity.

## Witness requirement

If a closure/quotient merges presentations, required provenance must either:

1. survive in an explicit witness; or
2. be declared intentionally lost.

Silent History erasure is forbidden.

## Consequence

A persistent carrier may have a coarse current state while retaining a richer recoverable identity in its witness/History structure.
