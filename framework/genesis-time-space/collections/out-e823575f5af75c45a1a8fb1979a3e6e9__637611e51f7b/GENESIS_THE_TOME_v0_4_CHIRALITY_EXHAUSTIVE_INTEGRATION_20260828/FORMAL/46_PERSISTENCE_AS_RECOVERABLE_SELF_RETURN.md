# 46 — PERSISTENCE AS RECOVERABLE SELF-RETURN

R14 supplies the structural T3→T5 persistence law.

## Definition 46.1 — T4 persistence

A T3 identity \(x\) is T4-persistent relative to an admitted redistribution \(r:x\to y\) iff there exists an admissible recovery History

\[
h:y\to x'
\]

with

\[
x'\sim_I x
\]

under the declared identity contract and with required provenance intact.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{\text{Persistence is recoverability of identity under admissible change.}}
\]

Persistence is not coordinate immobility.

## Definition 46.2 — T5 recovery saturation

A bounded domain reaches T5 persistent closure when its admitted internal redistribution family is recovery-saturated relative to the declared identity/witness contract.

Mutually recoverable presentations may then be quotiented into one persistent carrier identity, provided required History survives in witness.

## Structural interpretation

T4 is partial/asymmetric recoverability.

T5 is bounded recovery saturation plus witnessed closure.
