# 40 — CRADLE REACHABILITY AND RECURSIVE CLOSURE

R2 failed as a complete derivation but earned a foundational closure that is retained verbatim:

**Admissible recursive communication under cradle projection profiles generates a reachability closure satisfying the axioms of topological closure. The resulting closed domain can support non-trivial recursive dynamics.**

This result must not be weakened into “the Cradle proves a particular physics.”

## Definition 40.1 — Reachability closure

Let \(X\) be the current Cradle-state carrier and let \(\Gamma\) be the admitted recursive communication generators.

Define

\[
\operatorname{Reach}(S)
=
\{y:\exists x\in S,\exists\gamma\in\langle\Gamma\rangle,\gamma:x\to y\}.
\]

The closure operator is the admitted reachability closure, provided extensivity, monotonicity, and idempotence are satisfied by the declared generator system.

## Periodic recursion lock

A repeated return to the same presentation with retained nontrivial History is classified as:

**Periodic orbit under discrete dynamical recursion.**

The current state presentation and the History-bearing orbit are distinct data.

## Corollary 40.2

Recursive closure is compatible with nontrivial dynamics. Closure does not imply immobility.
