# 55 — PREIMAGE RECOVERY, HISTORY, AND HOLONOMY NO-GO RESULTS

R39–R42 sharpen the localization problem.

## S2 preimage recovery

For localized address \(p\), let \(F_p\) be the recovery-relevant decorated subset of \(\Pi^{-1}(p)\).

A retained witness \(W\) uniquely selects a representative iff its restriction to \(F_p\) separates every pair of distinct recovery-relevant lineages.

Full History is therefore a candidate discriminator, not an automatic proof of uniqueness.

## Torus winding no-go

Torus winding/net displacement does not faithfully encode ordered History.

Different ordered route words can have the same winding signature.

## Finite holonomy no-go

The Möbius/basin action has a concrete dihedral-type component satisfying the source relations

\[
T^6=\mathrm{id},\qquad
H^2=\mathrm{id},\qquad
HTH^{-1}=T^{-1}.
\]

But a finite dihedral element cannot encode an unbounded family of ordered History words.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{holonomy element}\neq\text{full ordered History}.}
\]

Concrete holonomy is downstream of connection-selected lifts.
