# PR-05 — Earned Primitives

**Book:** Anomaly Reports  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Date recorded:** 2026-08-29

## Record

Genesis extended Astraeus's foundational discipline to Vera: notation does not earn ontology. 'Let P be a path', 'let H be History', an equivalence symbol, a closure operator, or a classification may import unproved structure. Primitives may be explicitly admitted, but their admission and imported assumptions must be recorded. Paths, histories, classifications, and other structures must otherwise be constructed from previously admitted structure.
