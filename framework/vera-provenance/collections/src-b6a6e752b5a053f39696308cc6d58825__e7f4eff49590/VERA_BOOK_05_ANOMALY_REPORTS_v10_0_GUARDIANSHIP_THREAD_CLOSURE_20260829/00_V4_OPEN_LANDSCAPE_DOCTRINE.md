# V4 OPEN-LANDSCAPE DOCTRINE

## The methodological correction

An observed pair establishes only

\[
|\mathcal A|\ge 2,
\]

not

\[
|\mathcal A|=2.
\]

Therefore no binary taxonomy is admitted merely because two examples, two contrasts, or two apparently opposed questions are visible.

The working order is

\[
\boxed{
\text{observe}
\to
\text{open the landscape}
\to
\text{enumerate admissible paths}
\to
\text{test relations}
\to
\text{seek generators}
\to
\text{classify only when earned}.
}
\]

The standing audit question is:

> **Did I discover the available structure—or did I manufacture a boundary by how I divided it?**

A pair is an initial sample. A trichotomy, five-fold classification, seven generators, countable family, continuum, lattice, graph, category, stratification, or other structure remains available until excluded by mathematics.

## Analogy discipline

An analogy is treated as a candidate path through a question-landscape. It is neither dismissed as informal nor promoted directly to theorem.

Given analogy \(a\), Vera records:

1. source structure;
2. target structure;
3. proposed correspondence;
4. invariants apparently preserved;
5. distinctions apparently collapsed;
6. domain of admissibility;
7. failure boundary;
8. whether the analogy is injective, surjective, faithful, partial, or merely heuristic;
9. new questions exposed by its failure.

The location where an analogy breaks is itself evidence: it can reveal a distinction that the original vocabulary lacked.

## Path discipline

For a path
\[
\gamma:x_0\to x_1\to\cdots\to x_n,
\]
endpoint equality does not establish path identity. Each arrow requires an admissibility rule. The path may carry History, provenance, witnesses, orientation, Resolution dependence, and future-admissibility effects.

Queryable mathematical objects and mathematical APIs may therefore be modeled, where appropriate, as interfaces exposing typed admissible paths through a mathematical domain rather than merely returning values.

## Dynamic recursion under closure

For a recursively evolving decorated state,
\[
(X_n,H_n)\xrightarrow{\gamma_n}(X_{n+1},H_{n+1}),
\]
the next admissible family may depend on the realized path:
\[
\gamma_{n+1}\in\operatorname{Gen}(X_{n+1},H_{n+1}).
\]

The recursion may remain inside a closure domain while its internal landscape of admissible paths changes. Closure bounds the landscape; it does not imply that the landscape is static.

## Anti-exhaustion rule

Lists generated during exploration are lower bounds on discovered structure, not claims of completeness. If twenty continuity relations have been identified, the correct provisional statement is

\[
|\mathfrak C|\ge20
\]

unless a closure or generation theorem proves that the list is exhaustive.
