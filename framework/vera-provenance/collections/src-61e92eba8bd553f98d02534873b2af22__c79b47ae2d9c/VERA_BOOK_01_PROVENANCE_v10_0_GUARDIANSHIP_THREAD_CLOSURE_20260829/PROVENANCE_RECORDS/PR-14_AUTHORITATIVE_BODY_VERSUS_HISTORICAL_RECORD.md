# PR-14 — Authoritative Body versus Historical Record

**Book:** Provenance  
**Status:** ACTIVE GOVERNANCE RULE  
**Date recorded:** 2026-08-29

## Record

An earlier preservation proposal risked leaving mathematically wrong material inside the teaching sequence. Genesis corrected this. The authoritative chapter body must contain only mathematics currently judged correct/earned. Wrong, superseded, or unearned material is moved into provenance with original location, assumptions, reason for retirement, correction, and downstream impact. Nothing is erased, but authority is explicitly relocated.

## Book-specific relevance

This record belongs to the provenance of **Provenance** because the shared methodological development changed how this volume is authored, audited, or interpreted.

## Authority rule

This record is historical/provenance material. It does not override the current authoritative teaching body unless its status explicitly says the principle is active.

## Preservation

The record is retained even if later superseded. Later corrections append a new provenance record and link back rather than silently rewriting this record.
