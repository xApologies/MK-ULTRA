# V9.1 — Provenance integration

Provenance is extended from individual objects and theories to species-level inheritance.

A future human population inherits more than genes: it may also inherit environments, technologies, institutions, languages, mathematical structures, tools, and unresolved questions. These inheritance channels must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated notion of evolution.

Standing question:

\[
\boxed{\text{What does humanity inherit that changes what future humanity can become?}}
\]
