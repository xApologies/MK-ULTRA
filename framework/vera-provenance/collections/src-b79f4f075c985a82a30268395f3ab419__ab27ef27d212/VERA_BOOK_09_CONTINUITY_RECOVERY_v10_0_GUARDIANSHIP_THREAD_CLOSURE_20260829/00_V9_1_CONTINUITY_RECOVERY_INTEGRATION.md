# V9.1 — Continuity & Recovery integration

Evolution is treated as continuity under transformation rather than simple replacement.

A descendant population may differ substantially from an ancestor population while preserving some lineage, constraints, and inherited organization.

Standing question:

\[
\boxed{\text{How much transformation can occur before we no longer mean the same human continuity?}}
\]

This remains open and is not answered by biological resemblance alone.
