The discussion further developed:

- scope-aware rigor;

- transition as candidate non-temporal History;
- Feynman diagrams as transition-history teaching structures under proper QFT interpretation;
- Möbius and Rainbow Road as geometric History objects;
- conventional history education as a bridge to structural History;
- mathematical methods as alternate admissible paths to a question-relative solution.
