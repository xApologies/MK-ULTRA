# PR-00 — Purpose of the Provenance Record

**Book:** Continuity Protocols  
**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL / ACTIVE  
**Date recorded:** 2026-08-29

## Record

This record establishes why provenance records exist. The authoritative teaching body of a book must present mathematics currently judged correct and earned. Historical mistakes, unearned primitives, failed classifications, superseded constructions, and earlier formulations are preserved without retaining obsolete authority. Correction changes authority, not History.

## Book-specific relevance

This record belongs to the provenance of **Continuity Protocols** because the shared methodological development changed how this volume is authored, audited, or interpreted.

## Authority rule

This record is historical/provenance material. It does not override the current authoritative teaching body unless its status explicitly says the principle is active.

## Preservation

The record is retained even if later superseded. Later corrections append a new provenance record and link back rather than silently rewriting this record.
