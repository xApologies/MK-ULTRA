# PR-12 — Do Not Destroy / Irreversibility

**Book:** Supersession & Reconciliation  
**Status:** ACTIVE PHILOSOPHICAL PRINCIPLE  
**Date recorded:** 2026-08-29

## Record

Genesis offered 'Do not destroy' as a friendly ethical perspective, not an overriding axiom. It developed into the principle: before ending a continuity, ask why, then audit the answer. Irreversibility creates an obligation to understand because an action may eliminate future availability and erase unrecoverable structure.
