# Vera Provenance and Type Audit

## Rulings

1. **Same word is not same object.** Every domain use of `chirality` must receive a type signature.
2. **Same dimension count is not same space.** `3+1+1` may denote different typed coordinates across Time-Space, BRANE, manifolds, and applications.
3. **Representation is not object.** Serialized bytes, voxel arrays, ZIP layouts, labels, and colors may be views/telemetry rather than the underlying mathematical object.
4. **Implementation is real evidence of utility.** BRANE chirality/admissibility is executable architecture, even if no physical claim follows.
5. **Internal recovery is not external validation.** Atomic/ecology/CRD results remain internal until independent empirical criteria are met.
6. **No retrospective source inflation.** Later successful applications may motivate a common-grammar theorem but do not rewrite earlier sources as having proved it.
7. **No failure deletion.** Negative results and supersessions remain first-class database entries.
8. **Physics branches retain independent burden.** CRD→QCD and LIGO compatibility cannot be proved by the number of other domains using chirality.
9. **MMO and LIGO gaps stay gaps.** Do not narratively reconstruct missing source material.
10. **Roadmap is bounded search.** A failure at Xi remaps from the last closed lily pad.

## Current audit verdict

The corpus supports the proposition:

> Chirality is a recurring and, in some domains, implemented organizational abstraction across multiple project systems.

The corpus does **not yet** establish:

> Every occurrence is one mathematical invariant, or nature itself is fundamentally governed by that invariant.

That exact gap is the future project's subject.
