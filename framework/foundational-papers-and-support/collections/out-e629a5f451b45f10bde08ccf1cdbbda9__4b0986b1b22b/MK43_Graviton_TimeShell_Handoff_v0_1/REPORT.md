## 4. Exact geometry after the Time Shell

This is the explicit downstream calculation rather than another arrow diagram. Let h=Pi_T(q_*) be a supplied smooth tensor output in the declared classical branch. Let g=g_bar+h be nondegenerate and Lorentzian. Both metrics refer to the same manifold/patch. Denote their Levi-Civita connections by nabla_bar and nabla.

Define the connection difference K, distinct from the native continuity variable:

    K^ρ_μν := Γ^ρ_μν[g] - Γbar^ρ_μν.

Then

    K^ρ_μν = (1/2) g^ρσ
       (nabla_bar_μ h_νσ + nabla_bar_ν h_μσ
                         - nabla_bar_σ h_μν).         (13)

This identity is exact. The inverse is the full g^{-1}, not g_bar^{-1} unless a linear approximation is explicitly taken.

**Derivation.** Both connections are torsion-free, so K^ρ_μν is symmetric in μ,ν. Metric compatibility of nabla and nabla_bar gives

    nabla_bar_μ g_νσ = K^λ_μν g_λσ + K^λ_μσ g_νλ.

Add the expressions with μ and ν exchanged and subtract the expression with the σ derivative. Symmetry of K leaves 2 g_λσ K^λ_μν. Multiply by (1/2)g^ρσ and use nabla_bar g_bar=0. This proves (13).

The Riemann tensor follows:

    R^ρ_σμν[g] = Rbar^ρ_σμν
       + nabla_bar_μ K^ρ_νσ - nabla_bar_ν K^ρ_μσ
       + K^ρ_μλ K^λ_νσ - K^ρ_νλ K^λ_μσ.              (14)

**Derivation.** Substitute Γ=Γbar+K into the coordinate curvature formula. The pure background terms give Rbar, the cross and derivative terms combine into the background covariant derivatives of K, and the remaining products give the two quadratic terms. No expansion in small h is used.

Then

    Ric_σν = R^ρ_σρν,
    R = g^σν Ric_σν,
    G_μν = Ric_μν - (1/2)R g_μν.                     (15)

These are compositions of the metric readout with the original RP43 GR registry, EQ-GR-003 and EQ-GR-006–009. They supply the exact metric-to-curvature handoff once the native readout is evaluated. They do not extend a weak-field-only native projection into an unproved nonlinear projection. Exact geometry of a given g and exact microscopic validity of that g are different claims.

If the source provides only a first-order h, equations (13)–(15) can still evaluate the chosen g_bar+h exactly, but its higher-order curvature is not evidence that omitted second-order native metric terms vanish.
