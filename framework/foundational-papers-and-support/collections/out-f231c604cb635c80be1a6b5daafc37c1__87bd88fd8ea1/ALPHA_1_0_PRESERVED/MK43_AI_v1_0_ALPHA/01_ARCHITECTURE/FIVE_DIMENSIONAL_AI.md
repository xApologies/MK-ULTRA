# Five-Dimensional AI⁵ Model

```text
AI⁵ = (I, R, O, S, P)
```

- **Intent (I):** request semantics, requested operation, scope, audience, format, and success conditions.
- **Relevance (R):** ranking of handles, edges, branches, chunks, and candidate continuations.
- **Orientation (O):** chirality-aware direction, dependency orientation, explanatory ordering, and admissibility posture.
- **Recursive Strategy (S):** traversal budgets, branch expansion, termination, deferment, cycle handling, and continuation placement.
- **Decision Provenance (P):** reasons, inputs, scores, policy versions, rejected alternatives, and validation receipts.

The sixth coordinate is not stored canonically inside AI⁵. BRANE realizes AI⁵ request-relatively inside QMO⁶.
