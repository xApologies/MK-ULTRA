# Collapse Algorithm

Collapse converts the TreeRecoveryObject into an ordered finite realization for QMO projection.

Order:

1. root definition;
2. trunk or primary spine;
3. major branches by stable rank;
4. minor branches under their owning major branch;
5. terminal clusters;
6. equations and provenance adjacent to their owning nodes;
7. frontier and continuation map;
8. coverage and validation receipt.

Collapse may reorganize derived material but cannot rewrite canonical content.
