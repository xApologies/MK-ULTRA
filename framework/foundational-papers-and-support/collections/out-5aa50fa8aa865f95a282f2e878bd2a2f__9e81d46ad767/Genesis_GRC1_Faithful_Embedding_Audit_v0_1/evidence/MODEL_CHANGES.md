A future claim that the earlier theory embeds faithfully in this branch needs an actual construction, preservation of its equations/observables and an inverse. The present theorem does not assert it. Conversely, no such embedding is required to validate the explicit equivalence theorem for GRC-1 itself.

The central result is deliberately **equivalence by regular construction**. That is a legitimate way to build a reversible mathematical language. It is not evidence that a previously independent microscopic theory necessarily has Einstein dynamics.
