# Regulatory / Research Boundary

This Alpha intentionally defines two possible future product tracks without claiming either is already validated:

1. **Consumer accessibility / comfort research** — user-configured representations and environmental controls, subject to applicable consumer safety requirements.
2. **Clinical / rehabilitative research** — individualized profiles designed or supervised by qualified professionals with validated outcomes and the regulatory path appropriate to the claims.

A consumer prototype must not be relabeled as treatment evidence. A clinical claim requires external validation.
