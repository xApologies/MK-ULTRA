# Domain Boundary Guards

## Guard 1: R4 May Not Resolve

R4 cannot choose final stable topology.

R4 may only generate corridor candidates and elastic coupling fields.

## Guard 2: R5 May Not Pretend To Be R4

R5 cannot create corridors from nothing.

R5 must resolve corridors supplied by R4.

## Guard 3: R5 May Not Pretend To Be R6

R5 may resolve topology, but it does not certify long-term survivability.

## Guard 4: R6 May Not Rewrite Chirality

R6 stabilizes recoverability. It does not write chirality or invent new corridor candidates.

## Guard 5: No Element-Specific Shortcut

No domain may include hardcoded element rules such as:

- hydrogen always forms H2
- nitrogen always forms N2
- oxygen always forms O2

The output must arise from topology, chirality, bandwidth, strain, and recoverability.

## Guard 6: Preserve Ancestry

All domains must preserve recoverable ancestry unless a later experimental branch explicitly tests non-recoverable behavior.
