# Time Shell

A Time Shell does not replace Chirality.

A persistent Time-Shell domain localizes continuously updating Chirality into a persistent domain identity.

For a localized object \(X\) with Bandwidth \(B_X\),

\[
\chi_X^{(0)}\to\chi_X^{(1)}\to\chi_X^{(2)}\to\cdots
\]

may continue while the Time Shell preserves persistent organizational identity.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{\text{persistence}\neq\text{stasis}.}
\]

In the current conceptual organization:

- \(P\): time-independent domain;
- \(\neg P\): not-time-independent / Time-Shell domain.

The Time Shell supplies distinguished temporal continuation to a localized persistent domain while its underlying Chirality and History remain operative.
