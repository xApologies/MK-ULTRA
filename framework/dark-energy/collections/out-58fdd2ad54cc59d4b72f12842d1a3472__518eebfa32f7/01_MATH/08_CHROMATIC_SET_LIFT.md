# Shared-Resolution / Chromatic Seed → Byte Lift

Let a realized oriented seed carry two coarse basin occurrences \(a,b\in C\).

Do not create a new independent chromatic rule at the lift stage.

Use the ordered shared-Resolution states already present in the mathematics:

\[
\rho_+(a,b):=R[a,b]
\]

and

\[
\rho_-(a,b):=R[b,a].
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_R(a,b)
=
\big(
a,b,R[a,b],R[b,a]
\big).
}
\]

The coarse color readout is downstream:

\[
q_{\mathrm{col}}:\mathcal R_0\rightharpoonup C.
\]

Thus

\[
R[A,B]\neq R[B,A]
\]

may still satisfy

\[
q_{\mathrm{col}}(R[A,B])
=
q_{\mathrm{col}}(R[B,A]).
\]

History is lifted simultaneously, so reversed ordered relations remain distinct even under identical coarse color readout.

The exact seed→byte shared-Resolution lift is structurally closed.
