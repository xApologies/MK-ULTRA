# Void Ontology

Let \(D\) be a Chirality domain with address space \(B_D\).

Use a partial realized-state map:

\[
s_D:B_D\rightharpoonup X.
\]

Define the realized/existent domain

\[
E_D:=\operatorname{dom}(s_D)
\]

and the Void complement

\[
\boxed{V_D:=B_D\setminus E_D.}
\]

The Void is DNE / absence of realized structure.

For \(v\in V_D\),

\[
s_D(v)
\]

is undefined rather than assigned a numerical, chromatic, energetic, or hidden state.

The complement pattern and the realization/Void boundary are structural properties of the enclosing domain. This does not assign internal structure to the Void itself.

The Void describes the complementary landscape against which realized topography is discussed.
