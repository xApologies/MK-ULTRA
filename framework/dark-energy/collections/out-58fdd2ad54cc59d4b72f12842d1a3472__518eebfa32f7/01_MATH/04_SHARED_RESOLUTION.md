# Shared Resolution

Let the six Rainbow basin labels be

\[
C=\{R,O,Y,G,B,V\}.
\]

The ordered shared-Resolution pair space is

\[
\boxed{\mathcal R_0=C\times C}
\]

with \(36\) ordered profiles.

Write

\[
R[A,B]
\]

for the ordered shared-Resolution relation between an occurrence anchored in basin \(A\) and one anchored in basin \(B\).

Do not automatically identify

\[
R[A,B]=R[B,A].
\]

A multi-tile Resolution object may be represented graphically as

\[
\boxed{G_R(d)=(V,E_R,\rho_d)}
\]

with \(\rho_d(i,j)\) the ordered shared-Resolution profile across an admitted relation/boundary.

Recursive inherited Resolution History is

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_R=(G_R(0),G_R(1),\ldots,G_R(D)).
}
\]

Current closure identifies this inherited History object with the Resolution maintained by the relevant Bandwidth rather than treating Resolution depth as an independent primitive scalar.
