# Connection and Holonomy

The connection does not require a primitive scalar "greater depth wins" rule.

Because Resolution is the History structure maintained by a Bandwidth,

\[
R_B=H_B,
\]

the connection acts structurally on admissible Resolution/History classes.

Write schematically

\[
\nabla_B:\mathscr H_B\to\mathscr H_B
\]

or the appropriate path/category/groupoid generalization.

For a closed traversal \(\gamma\),

\[
\operatorname{Hol}_B(\gamma)
\]

records the History-sensitive residual after closed transport.

This closes the structural target and role of connection/holonomy.

A concrete physical/gauge representation of this structure remains downstream realization theory.
