# Toroidal Continuity

For one realized tile,

\[
\theta\in S^1.
\]

Lifted phase History:

\[
\Theta\in\mathbb R,\qquad \theta=\Theta\bmod2\pi.
\]

For two occupied tile phases,

\[
\boxed{
T^2=S_A^1\times S_B^1.
}
\]

The torus supplies continuity, orientation, cyclic traversal, reachability, winding, and History.

It does not uniquely select a realized path.

A coarse Rainbow color update may occur when continuous phase transport crosses a Resolution-sector boundary.

Thus color-update-by-sector-crossing is structurally closed as a candidate mechanism, while exact path selection/calibration remains representation/realization work.
