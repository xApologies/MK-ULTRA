# Mistakes, Drift, Recovery, and Supersession

This document records the major errors or over-promotions encountered during the closure crawl and how they were corrected.

## 1. `bite` terminology drift

Mistake:
- speech/model drift introduced `chirality bite`.

Correction:
- canonical term fixed to `chirality byte`, B-Y-T-E.

Recovery:
- all current QMO metadata uses `byte`;
- `bite` is retained only as retired provenance/alias history.

Impact:
- prevents creation of a false mathematical branch caused by transcription drift.

Impact:
- preserves canonical Zero/Cradle existence ontology.
