# Portal and Rainbow Road

Let \(E=\operatorname{dom}(s)\) and \(V=B\setminus E\).

The structural realization/Void boundary is

\[
\partial E=\partial V
\]

inside the enclosing address topology.

A Portal is a candidate admissible boundary-to-boundary communication relation:

\[
\boxed{
P_{\alpha\to\beta}:\partial E_\alpha\rightharpoonup\partial E_\beta.
}
\]

The Void itself remains DNE.

Rainbow Road is the global admissible Portal-road / information-topography structure.

A realized road may be written

\[
\gamma=P_n\circ\cdots\circ P_1.
\]

Native coexistence of admissible roads is not automatically a Hilbert-space quantum superposition.

Portal ontology/type is closed; exact physical Portal transport/calibration remains a downstream realization problem.
