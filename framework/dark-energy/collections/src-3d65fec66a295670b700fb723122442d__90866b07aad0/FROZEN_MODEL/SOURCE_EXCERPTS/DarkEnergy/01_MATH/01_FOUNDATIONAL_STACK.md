# Foundational Stack

## Genesis Field

The Genesis Field is the organizational landscape.

\[
G=\text{Genesis Field}.
\]

## Bandwidth

Current structural refinement:

> Bandwidth maintains/anchors an organizational domain as an object/domain in the Genesis Field.

For an organization \(X\),

\[
B_X
\]

denotes the Bandwidth maintaining the domain identified as \(X\).

This does not erase the historical Bandwidth prototype ("Available Organizational Freedom"); that earlier definition is preserved in provenance as historical lineage.

## History

History is an ordered, provenance-bearing organizational structure. Same endpoint/readout does not imply same History.

## Resolution

Current structural closure:

> Every Bandwidth maintains its own inherited organizational History; that maintained History is the Resolution of the organization supported by that Bandwidth.

Thus

\[
\boxed{R_B:=H_B.}
\]

Resolution is therefore not merely a scalar depth or a viewing choice.

### Time-independent domain

For a time-independent Bandwidth domain \(P\),

\[
\boxed{R_B^P=\mathscr H_B}
\]

where \(\mathscr H_B\) is the admissible History/path structure maintained by that Bandwidth.

Traversal of one path does not exhaust or destroy the remaining admissible History structure.

### Time-Shell domain

For a not-time-independent / Time-Shell domain \(\neg P\),

\[
\boxed{R_B^{\neg P}=H_B^{\mathrm{temporal}}}
\]

in the sense that the persistent localized organization now carries a distinguished temporal continuation while the underlying Chirality continues dynamically updating.

## Chirality

Chirality is the underlying relational organization/order grammar. It remains present inside Time-Shell objects.

A Time Shell does not replace Chirality.
