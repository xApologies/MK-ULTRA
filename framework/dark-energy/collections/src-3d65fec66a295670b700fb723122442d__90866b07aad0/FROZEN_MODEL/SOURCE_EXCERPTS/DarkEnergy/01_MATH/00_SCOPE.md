# Scope

\[
\boxed{\textbf{Let }G\textbf{ be the Genesis Field.}}
\]

All mathematical closure in this checkpoint is conditional on the admitted Genesis Field and its already-instantiated downstream domains.

This checkpoint does not attempt to derive the first event, the first realized Time-Space domain, or all of reality from DNE in every downstream theorem.

That is standard mathematical scoping: once an ambient structure is admitted, downstream theorems are proved relative to it.

A current Time-Space domain may be denoted

\[
T\subseteq G.
\]

A local Chirality state in that domain belongs to an admissible state space

\[
\Xi\in\mathcal X_T.
\]

The native update is therefore typed as a partial recursive map

\[
U_\chi:\mathcal X_T\rightharpoonup\mathcal X_T
\]

subject to the declared Bandwidth, Resolution, History, adjacency, persistence, Portal/Rainbow Road, and recursive-closure constraints.
