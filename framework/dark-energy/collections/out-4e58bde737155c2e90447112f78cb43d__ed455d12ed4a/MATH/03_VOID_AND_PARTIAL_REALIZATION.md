# Void and Partial Realization

Canonical statement:

> The Void is DNE: absence of realized structure.

The Void is not a hidden vacuum field, seventh basin, latent color, or internal SU(n) state.

Let `B_D` be an address domain. Use a partial realized-state map:

    s_D : B_D ⇀ X.

Define:

    E_D := dom(s_D)
    V_D := B_D \ E_D.

For `v in V_D`, `s_D(v)` is undefined rather than assigned an ordinary state value.

The Void itself contains no realized state. However, the complement pattern and the realization/Void boundary are structural facts of the enclosing domain.

Thus the Void describes the complementary landscape against which occupied topography is discussed without being a structured medium itself.
