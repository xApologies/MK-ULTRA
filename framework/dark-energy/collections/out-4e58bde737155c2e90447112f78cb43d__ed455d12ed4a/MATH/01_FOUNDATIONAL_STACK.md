# Foundational Stack

## Genesis Field
The Genesis Field describes the landscape in which organization may be realized and inherited.

## Bandwidth — current live refinement
Bandwidth is the organizational support/anchoring relation by which an object is maintained as an object in the Genesis Field.

Provisional notation:

    B_G(x)

Historical prototype definition: `Available Organizational Freedom`. Retained in provenance, not erased.

## Resolution — current live refinement
Resolution defines the relational occupancy/readout domain through which a Bandwidth-anchored organization is distinguishable.

Historical prototype definition: `Distinguishable Organization`. Retained in provenance.

## History / Provenance
History records inherited resolved states, relations, routes, and distinctions:

    H=(X_0,X_1,...,X_n).

Same endpoint/readout does not imply same History.

## Chirality
Chirality is relational organizational grammar across the realized domain; it is not merely handedness and is not identified with QCD color.

Current live conceptual stack:

    Genesis Field -> Bandwidth anchoring -> Resolution occupancy/readout -> History -> Chirality relational organization

This live refinement is kept separate from older source orderings rather than retroactively rewriting them.
