# Scope and Authority

Authority classes used in this checkpoint:

- `CURRENT`: adopted live terminology/structure.
- `SOURCE_CURRENT_R62`: current R62 source state.
- `SOURCE_CANDIDATE`: source-backed working mathematics (R36/R60/R61 and related work).
- `NEW_CANDIDATE`: formalized in the 2026-08-30 live crawl.
- `HISTORICAL_PROTO`: older design intent retained for provenance.
- `RETIRED`: superseded terminology or inference.

Nothing is promoted solely because it is aesthetically compatible with later work.
