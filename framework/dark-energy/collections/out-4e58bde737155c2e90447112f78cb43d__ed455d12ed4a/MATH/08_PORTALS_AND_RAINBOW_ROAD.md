# Portals and Rainbow Road

For partial realization:

    E=dom(s)
    V=B\E.

The realization/Void boundary is the structural interface between realized organization and DNE complement.

## Portal — new candidate typing

A Portal is provisionally an admissible boundary-to-boundary communication relation:

    P_{α→β}: ∂E_α ⇀ ∂E_β.

It does not require the Void to contain hidden state.

## Rainbow Road

Rainbow Road is the admissible information-topography / Portal-road structure across the Chirality Fabric.

One road:

    γ=P_n∘...∘P_1.

The global Road object is the currently admissible family/network of such compositions.

Native path multiplicity is not automatically Hilbert-space quantum superposition.

If realization changes, boundaries and Portal availability may change:

    E_t -> E_(t+1)
    ∂E_t -> ∂E_(t+1)
    RRoad_t -> RRoad_(t+1).

This is a candidate source of recursive context dependence.
