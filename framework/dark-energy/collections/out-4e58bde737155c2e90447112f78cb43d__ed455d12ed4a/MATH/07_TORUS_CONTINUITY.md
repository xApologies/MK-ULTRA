# Toroidal Continuity

## One occupied tile

    θ ∈ S¹.

Lifted phase History:

    Θ ∈ ℝ,
    θ=Θ mod 2π.

Continuous local transport:

    Θ -> Θ + δ.

A coarse basin update occurs when the resolved phase crosses a sector boundary. The equal-six-sector partition is a working symmetry model, not a canonical physical law.

## Seed torus

For two occupied tile phases:

    T²=S¹_A x S¹_B.

The six sectors on each factor yield 36 ordered coarse basin-pair cells.

Primitive local oriented tangent candidates before further constraints:

    {A+,A-,B+,B-}.

The torus supplies continuity, cyclic wraparound, reachability, orientation, winding, and History. It does not select the realized path.
