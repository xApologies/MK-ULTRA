## Remaining mathematical conditions
The following are not contradictions; they are the regularity conditions under which the finite Genesis structures possess the usual smooth GR representation:
- existence of the small-loop/Resolution curvature limit;
- nondegeneracy of the readable metric;
- existence of local source densities under refinement.

If the existing MK Ultra corpus already supplies stronger versions, those supersede these minimal conditions.
